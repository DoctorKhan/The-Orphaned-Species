## Canonical framing to carry forward

- **T1a cultural programming:** spectacle, atomization, control
- **Three Circles:** body → family → civilization; untying at each scale
- **Bird tribes:** T2 at civilization scale — flocking without command
- **Reciprocal circuit:** embodied + technical + relational accounts per decision
- **Counterfeit spirituality:** feel-good × imagination closed circuit; no third check
- **Technology teaches meaning:** runnable instruments, failed runs, verified protocols — not sermons
- **Phantom god-perception:** absent gods as domestication remote collar
