# Charkha Craft Lock

**Status:** Locked  
**Date:** 2026-08-06  
**Distinct from:** Maren's field notebook (parallel linguistic instrument, separate artifact)

## Core Identity

Primary: The Charkha is an organization — formally "the Charkha."  
Secondary: The Charkha can also be an artifact — a spoked wheel inscribed on a stone tablet.

Etymology traces to Circle/Chakra → spoked wheel → chariot culture.  
The organization preserves the knowledge of the wheel-tradition: the technology that carried PIE grammar across Eurasia and embedded the grammar of kneeling in conquered languages.

## Eli's Counter-Tension

Eli's name pulls back to El/Asherah (Canaanite substrate).  
He is outside the wheel-tradition's genealogy. The Charkha encodes a conquering grammar; Eli is the local god whose consort was written out of that grammar. This is built-in conflict.

## Secret-Society Layer

After chariot ceased to be a military monopoly, wheel-knowledge became guarded craft — guild, lodge, or encoded word-lineage. The spinning-wheel image (Gandhi's charkha) inverts the instrument of conquest into an instrument of independence. The Charkha operates in that inheritance.

## Relationship to Maren's field notebook

The Maren's field notebook is Maren's encoded journal — bounded sections unlocked by witnesses.  
The Charkha is a separate institution tied to the same wheel/etymology tradition. Both are linguistic decoding instruments; they run parallel tracks, not the same track.

## Guardrails

- The Charkha is not Maren's field notebook.
- It is not a sixth cosmological artifact.
- It is an interpretive matrix — organizational and material — rooted in chariot/wheel cultural memory.

## Supranational suzerainty reveal — LOCKED 2026-08-21

The Charkha is the hidden suzerain behind each government's psionic program. State programs are fronts, suppliers, and deniable theaters; the Charkha retains brokerage, transfer, custody leverage, and operational direction across Union, Mandate, and Coalition. The reveal lands in Book III and performs the Phase 1 → Phase 2 pivot: the conspiracy is not a secret society, it is a cartel that learned to write its own warrants.

**Mechanics:**
- Shared proprietary custody software, shell-company treasuries, and transfer authorizations appear across supposedly rival programs.
- Wren spots the pattern through intake-logistics anomalies and routing signatures she already knows how to read.
- Rowan spots it through the mass flow of human capital and psionic resources into the same control nodes.
- Rasel's Mandate participation collapses as justification: the program he believed was constrained and legitimate was a Charkha instrument from the start. His responsibility is real, but so is his status as manipulated asset.

**Escalation:** once Eli sees the Charkha suzerainty, running off the grid is impossible—there is no off-grid. The thriller engine shifts from fugitive-chase to insurgency/resistance. The villainy is not supernatural malice; it is ordinary human opportunism weaponizing an ancient control function.

**Cosmology bridge:** Charkha's fatal flaw is attempting to force a decentralized, entangled biological mesh into a top-down centralized command structure. The entropic blowback from that forced centralization is what Book V explains as structural design limit. The system buckles not because it is cursed, but because the Charkha tried to make it obey a single trunk.
