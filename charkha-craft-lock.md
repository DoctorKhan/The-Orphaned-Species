# Charkha Craft Lock

**Status:** Locked  
**Date:** 2026-08-06  
**Distinct from:** Rootbook (parallel linguistic instrument, separate artifact)

## Core Identity

Primary: The Charkha is an organization — formally "The Charkha Measure."  
Secondary: The Charkha can also be an artifact — a spoked wheel inscribed on a stone tablet.

Etymology traces to Circle/Chakra → spoked wheel → chariot culture.  
The organization preserves the knowledge of the wheel-tradition: the technology that carried PIE grammar across Eurasia and embedded the grammar of kneeling in conquered languages.

## Eli's Counter-Tension

Eli's name pulls back to El/Asherah (Canaanite substrate).  
He is outside the wheel-tradition's genealogy. The Charkha encodes a conquering grammar; Eli is the local god whose consort was written out of that grammar. This is built-in conflict.

## Secret-Society Layer

After chariot ceased to be a military monopoly, wheel-knowledge became guarded craft — guild, lodge, or encoded word-lineage. The spinning-wheel image (Gandhi's charkha) inverts the instrument of conquest into an instrument of independence. The Charkha Measure operates in that inheritance.

## Relationship to Rootbook

The Rootbook is Maren's encoded journal — bounded sections unlocked by witnesses.  
The Charkha is a separate institution tied to the same wheel/etymology tradition. Both are linguistic decoding instruments; they run parallel tracks, not the same track.

## Guardrails

- The Charkha is not the Rootbook.
- It is not a sixth cosmological artifact.
- It is an interpretive matrix — organizational and material — rooted in chariot/wheel cultural memory.
