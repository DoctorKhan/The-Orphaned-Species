## One-line

Kitchens, ports, and phones still run. The plants that used to print new
controllers, magnets, and throwaway airframes mostly do not. A drone in 2040
is a counted throw from a depot that will not refill at wartime speed.

---

## Why this file exists

`23` already forbids mass humanoid robotics: ordered metal pays a continuous
entropy bill, and late-2020s planners treated bodies like software. Military
drones were left in a separate register, as if a smaller airframe escaped the
same bill. It does not. Attrition doctrine *is* a replacement-rate claim. After
WWIII's hottest phase, the Fever Years, the chip and rare-earth burst, bloc
fracture, and a missing youth cohort, that rate has seized.

The wider collapse follows from the same physics. Anything that needs mid-stream
chemistry, leading-edge silicon, fresh magnets, or a twenty-year-old on a line
shares the drone's problem. Daily life can still cook. Complex refresh cannot
keep up.

**`23`'s "damaged but functioning" still holds for rooms people live in.** This
file names the industrial half of that sentence.

---

## Governing rules

1. **Daily life holds. Complex replacement seized.** People cook, sail, flirt,
   file papers, and bury their dead. A new flight controller, a new magnet lot,
   a new MRI tube, and a new throwaway airframe are political events.

2. **The seizure is sectoral and geographic.** Bulk freight, cement, desal,
   ship repair, older-platform medicine, welding, and diesel mechanics recovered
   along the quay. Headline growth at a Mandate or Coalition port can be real.
   The mid-stream stack behind the quay is allocated, hoarded, or dead.

3. **WWIII was the million-drone war.** Book I opens in armed reopening. The
   cheap-cloud era is already spent. What remains is leftover inventory,
   salvage, and one bloc that can still surge.

4. **A drone is a magazine item.** It appears, kills or misses, and is gone.
   After a surge the sky thins and people return to the doorway. Book III's
   drone war is Mandate and Union spending irreplaceable throws on a target
   they cannot delete.

5. **Field-sail and war spend the same remaining precision.** Every magnet lot
   Ma assigns to a corridor bird is a lot the ship, the grid, or a desal plant
   will not see this quarter. The peace engine and the assault are one budget.

6. **A registry query still runs on prewar silicon. A new motor has to be
   wound.**

## What still works

Use these as default 2040 texture. They fail locally, the way a pump fails.
They have not vanished as classes.

| Layer | What a body meets |
|---|---|
| **Food and water** | Markets, rice, fish, terrace cooking, salt in wells, monsoon fights, desal that runs because someone can swap a pump. Hungry weeks exist. Planetary famine as a single event does not. Book IV's The Hollow Year is a civilian face of the same seizure, already locked in `60`. |
| **Movement** | Scooters, bicycles, diesel trucks, river boats, sail, a few nursed aircraft on cannibalized parts. New car and jet fleets do not refresh. A working crane is a named object. |
| **Repair culture** | Welding, sewing, diesel mechanics, printed frames, pulled motors, dead battery packs treated as inventory (`66`). Eli's facilities work is the civic version of this layer. |
| **Medicine** | Sutures, antibiotics, older vaccine platforms, Somchai's clinic bags, a hoarded scanner that goes down for months. Licensing is the other bottleneck (`DECISIONS_Economic_Worldbuilding.md`). |
| **Software and paper** | Phones, remittance apps that stall, registries, mesh proofs, AI drill in empty school buildings. The informational layer is cheap. The clerk is still a person. |
| **Ports and bulk** | Quays, chandlery, Harbor Thread labor, thinner strait traffic than dashboards claim (`23` § *Performance vs. real throughput*). This is the Japan/West-Germany curve in `WORLD_BIBLE` § *Timeline*, real and confined to the quay. |
| **Human work** | Construction, perimeter standing, pump watch, drone operation while the magazine lasts, intake, care. Women already hold much of this. Culture still parks men in the last exposed hour (`33`). |
| **Installed sensors** | Perimeter stations, timing loops, face terminals, audit birds as *precious labs* (`64`). Already-built instrumentation keeps running until a part dies. |

## What seized

These are the dead or rationed classes. A prestige exception in the Stack or a
Continuity bunker is allowed. A street full of exceptions is not.

| Layer | What a body meets |
|---|---|
| **Leading-edge silicon** | 2020s chips in 2040 sockets. No civilian refresh cycle. Cherry Cube sells refurbished care as if it were new. |
| **Magnets, motors, packs** | Refining and magnet lines are the chokepoint (`66`). A denied component order, a held depot, three traceability stickers on one crate. |
| **Throwaway airframes** | FPV-class production cannot match WWIII throw-away rates. Workshops exist. They trickle. |
| **Humanoid and service robotics** | Already locked rare (`23` § *Robotics lock*). Same bill, larger island of order. |
| **Civil megaprojects** | Pelangi's Reclamation failed before scale. New towers are prestige speech. Dead elevator banks and dark HVAC floors sit inside shining glass. |
| **Aviation and auto refresh** | Fly and drive what remains. Cannibalize the rest. A parked airliner is a parts mine. |
| **High-end imaging and specialty medicine** | The tube is down. The reagent did not clear. A surgeon still cuts. |
| **Satellite density** | Gaps already locked. Terrestrial routes and stones matter again (`56`). |
| **Young skilled bench** | Missing cohort plus Fever plus early fertility decline in a minority of construct survivors. Assembly, night flying, fiber-spooling, and field repair are youth jobs sitting on a broken bench. |

**Audit drones** in `64` stay rugged mobile laboratories. They are counted
instruments, never expendable weather.

## How a place looks

**Meridian City / the Stack.** Casino Royale surface holds (`WORLD_BIBLE` §
aesthetic lock). Black cars, tailored crowds, one lobby humanoid, Circuit
drones as ceremony. Three floors above the intake hall sit dark because the
elevator controller died and the replacement lot is on hold. Cherry Lane is
still watered. Cherry Cube still glows. The care on offer is refurbished
stock and identity services. Harbor Thread still does the bodies.

**Pelangi Reach.** Functioning refuge in a dead resort shell (`23`). Pumps and
desal run because people maintain them. One patched quadruped on Babaji's
route. A new RO membrane is a rumor with a delivery date. Water stress is
ordinary. The king's robot-city pitch remains a poster.

**Malacca.** Reopening entrepôt: scooters, river diesel, heritage signs, empty
trishaws, one working crane. Chandlery can find a gasket. A flight controller
is a week and a favor.

**Union interior.** Continuity sites have stock and a guest list. Ordinary
towns have a closed plant used as a parts mine, a high-school gym used as a
depot, and cars that run until they do not. Devolution saved the roster. It
did not save the workshop.

**Mandate industrial cities.** Things still get made. Youth are claimed early.
Ma can surge a corridor or feed a shipyard. He cannot do both at magazine
depth in the same season. Migrant-worker losses remain under the "East
survived" story.

**Albion and broken-route Europe.** Corridor break already locked (`54`, Book
II Ch 6). Local repair, keeper memory, a thin official map.

## Drone magazines — LOCKED 2026-08-14

**Author-side doctrine.** Do not put the ratios in a character's mouth.

WWIII spent the cheap cloud. 2040 keeps drones as the main *machine* killer
wherever a magazine still exists. Most corridors, most weeks, the sky is empty
enough that a person still has to stand in a doorway.

| Bloc | Magazine | Failure |
|---|---|---|
| **Mandate** | A real surge: corridor strike, patrol, interceptors | The same quarter's ship, grid, or winter allocation |
| **Union** | Finite pre-war and Continuity stock | One broken plant or denied magnet lot and the cloud thins |
| **Ports** | Salvage frames, a few ISR treasures, civilian hulls | A lost motor is calendar, not weather |

**On-page rules**

- Sortie counts as speech. *Twelve this week. The depot is holding the packs
  for the ship.*
- Recoverable ISR is treasure. One-way strike is the cheap throw, and even
  that is counted.
- Counter-drone is nets, rifles, spoofing, a jammer, a kid on a roof. A second
  infinite cloud does not answer the first.
- Workshop texture: pulled motors, swollen packs, a denied component order.
- After a surge, people return to exposed jobs because the sky emptied. That
  is how Book III male scarcity *begins*. Culture still parks men in the
  doorway. Industry no longer has a machine to stand there instead.
- A strike that kills the man who came to ask costs a named life and a bird
  someone will not get back.

**Autonomy** remains the hard counter to a Block (`38` §3a). Under seizure it
is also the expensive counter. Seekers, radios, and chips are the part a garage
cannot print. The rational leftover stock is dumber and more human-flown until
the humans run out too. Then you get half-blind birds on a poisoned list.

**Psions and magazines.** A false signature that sends six birds into empty
water is a factory defeat. A Block on a launch officer saves a throw. Psions
fight operators and data (`38` §3a). They lose the strike that has nobody
home, when that strike still has a bird left.

## Warfare balance — author-side, LOCKED 2026-08-14

These are planning ratios. They are not on-page pie charts.

| Layer | Everyday 2040 | What it decides |
|---|---|---|
| **Drones** | Scarce, loud when they appear, then gone | A corridor, a ship, a named death |
| **Holding, repair, extraction** | Most of the hours called war | Who lives in the doorway after the magazine empties |
| **Bio** | Already fired. Clinic, roster, early fertility, workshop-age bodies | Who exists to assemble or fly anything |
| **Psionics** | Tiny headcount | Whether the last operator launches; whether the list is true |

The stack, tightened:

The Fever removed workshop-age bodies → registries turn survivors into
coordinates → drones spend the last cheap order against those coordinates → a
Block or decoy wastes industrial irreplaceability → planners want the mind off
the trigger → autonomy needs the chips and sensors the late-2020s burst and
`66` already rationed → half-blind leftover stock, and people back in the open.

A second engineered pandemic is refused. Bio's afterlife is law, clinic, and
who is left to staff a bench.

Nuclear restraint is unchanged (`99` § *nuclear restraint*). A strike can
murder a cluster. It cannot end a distributed, embedded, still-emerging trait,
and it cannot restock a magnet line.

## Craft

1. **One industrial scar per scene** — a dark floor, a held pack, a denied
   lot, a counted sortie, a cannibalized van. Same ceiling as `23`.
2. **Procedure, not briefing.** Wren reads a manifest. Eli waits on a gasket.
   Ma signs an allocation. A clerk says they cannot get parts (`23` already
   has the line).
3. **Prestige is loud because it is rare.** A humanoid, a Circuit bird, a
   working MRI, a fresh magnet crate. Locals stare.
4. **Do not empty the kitchen to prove the plant died.** Markets, jokes,
   bad gardens, and flirtation stay. The seizure lives in the spare-parts
   drawer.
5. **Term budget.** Author: *replacement seizure*, *magazine*, *depot hold*.
   Street: *we can't get parts*, *the depot is holding*, *twelve this week*,
   *pull it from the wreck*. Do not say *replacement seizure* in dialogue
   unless a Metrology or allocator official is speaking on purpose.

---

## Guards

- No Mad Max continent. No robot infantry. No million-drone sky in 2040.
- No second Fever as a plot engine.
- No equal collapse in every bloc. Mandate still makes things. Union still
  launches what is in the bunker. Ports still eat and sail.
- No software prosperity that quietly restocks the magnet line.
- No new chapter plots. This file changes texture, budgets, and the reading
  of existing assaults.
- `23` § *What did not change* still binds: paper, cash, diesel, knives,
  phones, hospitals, and real places with 2040 texture.
