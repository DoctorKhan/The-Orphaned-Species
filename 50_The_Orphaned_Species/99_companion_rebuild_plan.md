# Companion Rebuild Plan — Scoped

**Scope lock first, prose second.**

Companions are Layer 2. They document the system the novels dramatize.
They do not replace the novels, sermonize, or invent cosmology.

---

## Scope lock — FINAL 2026-08-05

- **Audience:** novel readers first, practitioners second, general audience third.
- **Tone:** investigative companion + practical field guide. Not fiction, not philosophy, not lecture.
- **Length:** one focused volume each, ~40–70k words. Appendices carry evidentiary weight; main text carries choice, cost, and practice.
- **Deliverable contract:** each companion opens with a scene the reader could step into; closes with a practice the reader could use; never opens with cosmology, theory, or taxonomy speech.
- **Non-negotiable:** no companion may solve what the novels are still testing. If a passage does not create choice, cost, or relationship change, it belongs in Layer 3 (the Record), not in a companion.

---

## Canonical framing to carry into every companion

- **Three-Term Model:** T1a cultural programming, T1b family/individual programming, T2 what you do.
- **Three Circles:** body → family → civilization; untying technique at every scale.
- **Bird tribes:** T2 at civilization scale — flocking without command, reorganization by shared attention.
- **Reciprocal circuit:** embodied account + technical account + relational account per consequential decision.
- **Counterfeit spirituality:** feel-good × imagination closed circuit; no third check; empowerment without cost.
- **Technology teaches meaning:** runnable instruments, failed runs, verified protocols — not cosmology sermons.
- **Phantom god-perception:** perceiving absent gods as domestication remote collar; mature control-availability payoff deferred to V+.

## Companion skeleton

### The Human Experiment
- **Layer:** origin
- **Job:** explain the makers, the orphaning, the planes, and the origin of the bridging capacity.
- **Structure:**
  - Part I — The orphaning: what was done and why
  - Part II — The planes: body, family, civilization as nested programming
  - Part III — The response: how the bridging capacity was preserved and transmitted
  - Appendices — evidence floor, chronology, glossary
- **Retired terms to avoid:** Werdh-anes, Reges, Wiros, Alba, Gneh-tah, cosmic betrayal, domestication program as master plot.
- **Opening move:** start with the body, not the stars. The reader’s lived experience of programming comes first.

### The Social Game
- **Layer:** T1a cultural programming
- **Job:** diagnose the local attractor as spectacle, atomization, and control — not Monopoly Mechanism, Kin vs Machine, Four Spheres, or Hollow Middle.
- **Structure:**
  - Part I — The game as given: spectacle, competition, separation
  - Part II — The rules visible in institutions: education, medicine, media, law
  - Part III — The exit move: mutual aid, consent-bound coordination, compact without merger
  - Appendices — civic pattern library, case studies, glossary
- **Retired framing to remove:** Monopoly Mechanism, Kin vs Machine, Networked Ark, Four Spheres of Belonging.
- **Opening move:** start with a lived social situation, not a theory of civilization.

### Manual Override
- **Layer:** T2 at body scale
- **Job:** teach the untying practice — breath, stillness, healing touch, dream, plant-mind — and diagnose T1b at family scale.
- **Structure:**
  - Part I — The body as conditioned instrument
  - Part II — The family tether: release without shame
  - Part III — Practice sequences: breath, threshold, contact, dream
  - Appendices — practice logs, safety rules, glossary
- **Terminology pass:** autopilot / NPC / Player / Manual Override / release / threshold / contact.
- **Opening move:** a first-person somatic experience, not a cosmology lecture.

### The Cosmic Game
- **Layer:** T2 at civilization → infinite scale
- **Job:** Bridge → past lives → Ω; lucid creation; convergence.
- **Structure:**
  - Part I — The Bridge: the compact at infinite scale
  - Part II — The past lives as practice lineage, not identity recovery
  - Part III — Ω: convergence without ownership; lucid creation
  - Appendices — Bridge forms, convergence glossary, field notes
- **Retired framing to remove:** Consciousness Technologies, spiritual technology, activation as goal.
- **Opening move:** start with civic practice, not cosmic claim.
