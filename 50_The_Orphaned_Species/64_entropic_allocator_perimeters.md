## How perimeter law arose — Entropy Crimes

**Entropy Crime** began as a legal doctrine before it became the organizing
principle of the allocator. It was born in the settlement after a mid-2030s
conventional-cyber-**stable-coin** war: the United States won the financial front,
but global opinion had turned against the scale of concealed burden it had
exported through that same payment and identity infrastructure. The resulting
statutes and perimeter system were deliberately designed to constrain the kind of
cross-border cost-hiding American-led operators had used to win. The Continental
Union, as the American successor state, rejected both the Entropy Crimes regime
and the blame narrative that came with it, isolating itself legally and
politically while inheriting the older American industrial and military footprint.

**Formalization after the Weapon — LOCKED 2026-08-10:** rejection did not hold.
The Weapon's male-skewed mortality left a female political majority across most
surviving populations, and that majority finished what litigation alone could
not: it converted Entropy Crimes from a doctrine a large violator could decline
into a binding global regime enforced through the allocator mesh. The
institutions built to answer to a male-majority electorate could not survive
that shift intact. Continental Union's predecessor states fractured under the
pressure rather than ratify; other nations broke along the same line. What
reconsolidated after — Continental Union, Eastern Mandate, Free Ports Coalition
— left no single successor state large enough to dominate the settlement again.
Full timeline: `23_2040_present_frame.md` § *Macro timeline*.

Late-2020s and early-2030s courts already faced abandoned
buffer tanks, shell-company waste transfers, falsified supply provenance,
deliberate heat shedding during grid emergencies, and occupational damage moved
off a factory's books. Existing environmental, labor, property, and bankruptcy
law divided one physical burden among separate cases. Each paper entity could
appear compliant while the harm continued downstream.

### Data-center catalyst — LOCKED 2026-08-09

AI data centers supplied the catalytic Entropy Crime cases. During an
early-2030s heat, water, and grid emergency, protected compute campuses kept
running while nearby districts accepted rationing and rolling outages. Their
carbon dashboards remained green. Renewable contracts and corporate accounting
hid local cooling demand, waste heat, backup generation, rapid chip turnover,
and burdens shifted among related operators.

Investigators found that compute and liability could move between campuses and
shell companies faster than environmental or labor claims followed them. Each
site reported a compliant fragment. The cooling basin, grid, and neighboring
households received the combined cost.

The resulting litigation forced a durable bargain. Regulators sought internal
logs to establish what the campuses had run and when. Operators invoked trade
secrecy, client confidentiality, and security. The courts held that the workload
could remain private if the operator proved the physical cost at a registered
boundary:

> The public has no claim on what you compute. It has a claim on what your
> computation costs the commons.

Data centers became the first mature perimeter pilots. They already had secure
facilities, dense environmental instrumentation, standardized service points,
cryptographic staff, and strong reasons to prove compliance without revealing
operations. Their first EEU profiles separated grid draw, cooling-water burden,
heat discharge, backup exhaust, material turnover, and occupational exposure.
The privacy-preserving proof architecture developed there before spreading to
power plants, ports, factories, ships, farms, and living institutions.

The allocator AI descends from the remedy built after AI infrastructure exposed
the accounting failure. It remains a public scientific utility because the
founding cases showed what happened when private computation could describe its
own costs without an independently measured boundary.

**Locked scope:** AI data centers are the catalyst and first mature pilots. The
jurisdiction, campus, case name, corporate defendants, triggering incident, and
human cost remain open for later plot use.

The new doctrine named a common act: knowingly concealing, exporting, assigning,
or laundering a consequential burden across a protected boundary without lawful
custody. The statutes targeted human conduct surrounding the physical burden.
Entropy itself remained an unavoidable condition of life and work.

Early Entropy Crime cases established four rules that later produced the
perimeter system:

1. **The burden follows the asset.** Sale, insolvency, restructuring, and salvage
   cannot clear stored physical liability.
2. **A transfer requires custody.** Waste, heat, hazardous material, and embedded
   production burdens leave one account only when another lawful account accepts
   them.
3. **Bodies have standing.** Occupational harm carried home by a worker crosses
   a protected perimeter even when the factory's exhaust record is clean.
4. **Measurement earns privacy.** An operator that proves its registered
   crossings keeps its interior processes sealed. Concealment opens the fence.

The perimeter became the practical answer to an evidentiary problem. Courts
needed a place where custody changed hands and measurements could be challenged.
Industry wanted protection for process secrets. Labor and ecological claimants
wanted burdens to remain attached to the parties that created them. Insurers and
lenders wanted a balance sheet that survived bankruptcy. The registered
boundary gave each group something enforceable.

After the Turning, blocs adapted the doctrine to ration scarce industrial capacity.
Legacy emissions monitors, port inspections, occupational exposure systems, and
grid controls became the first perimeter stations. Closure bonds grew from
bankruptcy judgments. Gradient passports grew from supply-chain and smuggling
cases. Bonded interconnects grew from industrial districts that needed a lawful
way to exchange waste and recovery capacity.

Strict boundary liability pays for containment, repair, and compensation when a
crossing causes harm. An **Entropy Crime** requires an additional finding of
knowledge, fraud, recklessness, coercion, or deliberate bypass. Equipment failure
reported promptly may create liability and an operating pause without creating a
criminal conviction.

Recognized Entropy Crimes include:

| Offense | Conduct |
|---|---|
| **Ghost discharge** | Routes a consequential flow through an unregistered pipe, vent, conduit, or diffuse outlet |
| **Liability concealment** | Hides stored heat, waste, ecological depletion, or human recovery debt |
| **Custody laundering** | Assigns a burden to a perimeter that did not knowingly accept it |
| **Proof fraud** | Forges sensor evidence, gradient provenance, closure records, or a compliance proof |
| **Boundary fraud** | Draws, divides, or relabels a perimeter to strand the burden outside the responsible account |
| **Sovereign-perimeter violation** | Treats a person's body or protected living capacity as transferable industrial property |
| **Metrology sabotage** | Corrupts a reference instrument, audit station, or trust identity to damage another perimeter |

The doctrine remains politically vulnerable. The Continental Union expands
Entropy Crime labels while narrowing the exemptions enjoyed by ordinary people.
Mandate ministries turn accusation into loss of clearance before a criminal case
is heard. Free Ports often demand visible harm and witnessed custody, which
protects against remote prosecution and lets sophisticated ghosting survive
longer.

## Governing axiom

A registered perimeter receives operational privacy for transformations within
it and bears strict liability for consequential flows across its boundary.
Living bodies retain sovereign perimeters inside every larger enclosure.

The fence, hull, pressure boundary, watershed edge, or district outlet is the
legal interface between private operation and the shared thermodynamic commons.
The allocator does not need an arc furnace's recipe or a ship engineer's shift
log. It needs trustworthy measurements of what crosses the registered boundary
and an honest account of liabilities still stored inside it.

Allocator privacy covers production methods, internal routing, timing, and trade
secrets. Fire codes, labor rights, structural safety, and the bodily sovereignty
of people inside continue to apply. A factory fence cannot turn workers into
internal equipment.

## What a perimeter can enclose

A perimeter may enclose one forge, a factory complex, a ship, a district heating
plant, a farm, a threshold house, or an industrial quarter. Large systems contain
nested perimeters with separate rights and accounts:

```text
worker's body
  inside a workshop
    inside a factory
      inside an industrial district
        inside a city, watershed, and bloc
```

Each registered crossing point has a declared function. Cooling water, exhaust,
electrical feeds, cargo gates, worker gates, sewage lines, and bonded waste pipes
belong to the boundary map. An unregistered crossing is a breach even when its
measured discharge would have fit the budget.

Perimeter maps cannot be redrawn through an owner's declaration. Expansion,
division, merger, and annexation require public notice and review by every
jurisdiction that inherits the resulting risk. Bodies and watersheds keep their
own standing when a larger industrial perimeter encloses them.

## Ownership, custody, and theft

Traditional ownership survives in 2040. People may own homes, tools, vehicles,
ships, businesses, and industrial equipment. A title grants control over an
asset within the law. It does not grant ownership of another body, permanent
title to a commons, or immunity from consequences that cross a perimeter.

The civic distinction is between owning an asset and owning everything the asset
touches. A person may own a workboat. The crew retain bodily sovereignty, the
sea remains a commons, and the hull's registered perimeter remains liable for
its discharge.

### Forms of ownership

| Thing | Legal form | Governing limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Body, identity, and medical data** | Sovereign self-possession | Cannot be sold, inherited, pledged, or absorbed into an employer's property |
| **Clothes, tools, furniture, and ordinary vehicles** | Private ownership | Ordinary safety, tax, and perimeter law |
| **Homes** | Private, household, cooperative, or trust title | Occupancy, tenancy, inheritance, and displacement protections |
| **Shops and small businesses** | Private or cooperative title | Labor duties and perimeter liability follow control |
| **Factories, ships, and regulated machinery** | Registered title | Stored liability, closure bonds, and certified custody follow the asset |
| **Land** | Title with stewardship duties | Air, water, wildlife, ecological flows, and ley routes retain independent standing |
| **Critical infrastructure** | Public, cooperative, or chartered private title | Service obligations, audit, and limits on closure |
| **EEU allocation** | Time-limited operating license | Cannot become a perpetual or inherited right to burden the commons |
| **Threshold houses and cloisters** | Trust or cooperative custody | Residents keep exit and occupancy rights; stewards cannot sell the community for private gain |
| **Life technology and psionic capacity** | Unownable living capacity | Instruments may be owned; people, capacities, practices, and historical imprints cannot |

Ownership of land does not erase overlapping ecological perimeters. A deed may
control a field's ordinary use while a watershed account governs its water and a
living-route trust protects a conductor beneath it. These claims overlap. None
automatically consumes the others.

### Allocation is permission

An EEU profile is a public operating permission attached to a registered
perimeter and accounting period. It is not part of the land's permanent title.
Class-specific surplus may trade through the Bourse under geographic and time
limits. Unused permission expires or decays under the applicable public rule, so
a wealthy operator cannot accumulate an inherited right to burden the commons.

Human recovery capacity never belongs to an employer and never enters the
Bourse. Ecological recovery enters a tradable account only after observed repair
clears its reversal period.

### Transfer of regulated property

A sale of regulated land, a ship, or an industrial facility requires:

1. a current perimeter proof;
2. a certified inventory of stored liabilities;
3. transfer or replacement of the closure bond;
4. the buyer's witnessed acceptance of custody;
5. a challenge period or inspection for concealed burdens.

The seller remains answerable for fraud and undisclosed waste discovered after
transfer. The buyer assumes declared operating liabilities at closing. Dividing
a company, changing a hull identity, or moving an asset into a new trust does not
clear its physical account.

### Proof of title

Ordinary possessions do not enter a universal registry. Locks, receipts, maker's
marks, repair history, and witnesses establish everyday ownership. Homes, land,
ships, regulated machinery, and other high-value assets use redundant title
records: a local registry, a bloc ledger, a paper instrument, and a witnessed
archive. The loss or capture of one record cannot erase the claim by itself.

Certified perimeter components have serial identities, physical seals, and
installation records. A Marshal or licensed mechanic pairs a high-risk cartridge
to its perimeter when it is installed. A stolen component can still be altered
or rehoused, but it cannot clear an ordinary challenge, certified repair,
insurance claim, port inspection, or Bourse transfer without forged custody.

This system raises the cost of selling stolen property. It does not make theft
impossible. Grey repair shops, corrupt registrars, false witnesses, altered hull
identities, and forged provenance remain active trades.

### Theft and remedy

The law distinguishes several property harms:

| Harm | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Theft** | Taking property or lawful custody without authority |
| **Conversion** | Diverting entrusted property to an unauthorized use |
| **Title fraud** | Selling, pledging, or registering an asset without a valid claim |
| **Custody breach** | Diverting cooperative, trust, or bonded property for private use |
| **Perimeter theft** | Charging discharge or stored liability to another perimeter |
| **Commons theft** | Enclosing water, routes, living capacity, or knowledge that cannot lawfully become private property |

Restitution comes first: return, repair, replacement, or compensation. Violence,
organized fraud, repeated predation, and abuse of entrusted custody carry
criminal penalties. Strict boundary liability funds containment and repair after
an accident. Criminal guilt requires a further finding such as concealment,
recklessness, coercion, or deliberate bypass.

### Displacement, abandonment, and salvage

Emergency occupation does not immediately erase an absent owner's title. It also
does not require usable homes to remain empty during mass displacement. Local
courts can grant temporary occupancy, record maintenance and perimeter costs,
and hear an abandonment claim after the jurisdiction's waiting period.

When an owner or heir returns, the court weighs the title record, reason for
absence, care supplied by the occupants, improvements made, and the cost of
another displacement. Reclamation may require compensation, shared title, a
replacement home, or continued tenancy.

Salvage follows the same principle. Recovery of an abandoned machine or vessel
can create a possessory claim after notice and inspection. Removing its identity
plate does not establish abandonment, and its stored liabilities survive the
salvage transfer.

## The budget is a vector

**Entropic allocation** is the civic name for a family of measured burdens. An
allocation does not pretend that heat, arsenic, topsoil, and heat-strained bodies
are naturally interchangeable. Each perimeter receives a multidimensional
**EEU profile** whose components have their own limits, transfer rules, and
appeal standards.

| Account | Boundary evidence |
|---|---|
| **Thermal** | Enthalpy and temperature of incoming and outgoing flows; radiative and convective loss |
| **Chemical** | Mass, persistence, and hazard class of compounds crossing |
| **Material** | Ordered inputs, recoverable products, mixed outputs, and stranded components |
| **Vibrational** | Ground-borne or water-borne energy above local exposure thresholds |
| **Ecological** | Water quality, soil depletion, biomass loss, reproductive capacity, and recovery rate |
| **Human** | Occupational heat strain, exposure, injury, recovery debt, and loss of functional capacity |
| **Biological production** | Calories, biomass, disease load, fertility effects, and mortality within the relevant population |

The public may speak of **Entropy Emission Units** or **EEUs** as a collective
term. The ledger retains the component underneath. Unused thermal capacity does
not purchase permission to poison a river or exhaust a workforce.

Budgets reflect regional limits as well as physical measurement. The same heat
discharge has different consequences beside a cold sea, inside an urban heat
island, and upstream of a stressed estuary. Measurement stays falsifiable;
allocation remains a political decision made in public.

## Perimeter sensor stations

Every matter or energy crossing named on the boundary map terminates at a
mandated sensor cluster. A heavy industrial station typically includes:

- matched thermocouples and mass-flow meters on cooling loops;
- optical or colorimetric sampling on flues and liquid discharge;
- archived particulate filters with witnessed replacement dates;
- geophones where presses, excavation, or mining create ground-borne load;
- independent primary and secondary instruments feeding separate sealed logs.

Thermal cameras and mobile sniffers watch for diffuse losses between declared
crossings. Small perimeters use simpler certified packages. A workboat may carry
one sealed exhaust dosimeter, a cooling-water flow pair, and tamper tape across
its discharge fittings. A district-scale perimeter operates several sheds and
publishes a proof that aggregates them.

Operators may instrument every machine inside for maintenance or efficiency.
Those readings remain legally invisible to the allocator until a breach audit
revokes perimeter privacy. The official account begins at the boundary station.

## Physical enforcement

The digital ledger records rights and evidence. Physical components make
continued operation costly to fake. Certified boundary hardware includes
thermal-dose capsules, sealed valves, fatigue coupons, reactive exhaust tags,
shear-pins, and replaceable chemical cartridges. Components melt, clog, discolor,
or expose a witness mark after a calibrated burden.

The state controls stamped replacements for high-risk classes. A perimeter may
have EEU headroom on the ledger and still remain dark while its operator waits
for a certified valve cartridge. Reserve facilities receive priority supply;
Free Port mechanics recover housings and improvise around incompatible bloc
standards. The supply depot remains a place where policy becomes physical.

Internal machines may use voluntary limiters to protect the perimeter budget.
Their readings assist the operator and carry no direct regulatory standing.

### Spent glass

**Spent glass** is the street term for a sealed colorimetric dosimeter mounted at
a registered exhaust, flue, or liquid-discharge fitting. The reactive element
darkens irreversibly under cumulative exposure. Clear or green glass retains
capacity; progressive darkening warns that replacement or inspection is near;
black glass is spent, overloaded, or evidence of exposure outside its certified
class.

Surface dirt does not change the reading. A clerk wipes the sight clean and
checks the color beneath. Sawing off the housing, bleaching the witness layer,
or replacing it without a stamped custody record leaves visible tamper evidence.
A workboat may carry spent glass at its exhaust and cooling-water outlets.

Spent glass is a cheap warning, cumulative physical witness, and checkpoint
object. It does not replace the integrated perimeter record. The certified
sensor cluster and its compliance proof remain authoritative.

## Privacy, proofs, and the allocator mesh

Each perimeter has a tamper-resistant Audit Coprocessor between its official
sensors, sealed logger, and bloc allocator mesh. At voted intervals, the
allocator sends a random challenge. The coprocessor returns a compact
zero-knowledge proof that:

1. the station firmware matches a Temple-certified image;
2. redundant sensors agree within their calibrated bounds;
3. the integrated totals remain within the perimeter's current EEU profile;
4. declared stored liabilities reconcile with the previous accounting period.

The public ledger receives the proof, perimeter identity, applicable model
version, and audit status. Minute-by-minute production patterns stay private.
A jurisdiction may publish a boolean result or a coarse budget band. The choice
belongs to public policy, with emergency disclosure rules set in advance.

A proof establishes that certified instruments produced a compliant record. It
does not calibrate its own instruments. Drone audits and Metrology Temples close
that loop.

Three official industrial meshes carry these proofs: UnionNet, MandateNet, and
PortsNet. A separate dark mesh carries unrecognized house audits, grey-market
claims, and stolen provenance. Consumer communication can cross bloc lines;
allocator identities and trust streaks do not transfer without reconciliation.

## Drone audits and Metrology Temples

An audit drone is a rugged mobile calibration laboratory. It docks at the
perimeter shed and applies known thermal loads, calibration gases, optical
references, and vibration signals through a standard test manifold. Shielded
onboard instruments compare those references with the station record. The drone
posts a proof of the comparison without transmitting the perimeter's raw time
series.

A human Audit Marshal controls entry, seals replaced components, writes a
carbon-copy affidavit, and can stop an unsafe test. The Marshal also walks the
boundary with thermal imaging, chemical sampling, and subsurface survey tools.
An unexplained plume or buried line can trigger a breach audit.

Metrology Temples maintain the reference instruments, firmware hashes, proof
circuits, and calibration chains. Their reports publish uncertainty, known
failure modes, recalls, and dissenting measurements. A fleet-wide drift can
invalidate months of proofs and force a public decision about restitution and
re-audit.

Audit frequency follows risk and trust. Clean, low-hazard perimeters receive
fewer physical visits. Missed challenges, unexplained sensor disagreement,
rapid boundary changes, and prior breaches raise the cadence. Network failure
creates an amber condition and a conservative provisional estimate before it
creates a criminal presumption.

## Breach audit and loss of privacy

A failed calibration proof does not reveal the interior automatically. It gives
the operator a short period to challenge the reference, stop the affected
outlet, or accept a witnessed retest. Repeated failure, an unregistered crossing,
or evidence of deliberate spoofing authorizes a breach audit.

During a breach audit, a human team may inspect internal process equipment,
buffer tanks, worker-safety records, and undocumented conduits related to the
discrepancy. The warrant states its scope and expires. A proven hidden outlet
can place the perimeter under full-disclosure supervision for a year.

Killing or coercing a Marshal is a major crime and raises local audit security.
District-wide collective punishment is a Continental Union capture practice,
not a founding feature of the democratic system.

## Internal entropy management

The perimeter gives operators room to solve their own engineering problem.
Factories invest in molten-salt tanks, borehole storage, scrubber beds,
precipitators, separable alloys, heat recovery, and shift schedules built around
the boundary budget. A plant that recovers more useful work from the same inputs
releases less burden and earns a class-specific surplus.

Buffers delay a crossing. They do not cancel the stored liability. Every account
period begins with the prior period's certified inventory:

```text
opening stored liability
+ new captured burden
- verified recovery or lawful custody transfer
- measured release through the perimeter
= closing stored liability
```

Closing liability carries forward when the monthly operating allowance resets.
A hot tank discharged next month consumes next month's thermal allowance. A bed
of captured solvent remains on the books until recovery, treatment, or bonded
transfer clears it. Buffer capacity and containment risk appear in the public
proof even when operating details remain sealed.

Stored liabilities outrank ordinary creditors. An operator must post a closure
bond sufficient to cool, remove, or stabilize them after insolvency. Without
that rule, the cheapest business model would fill the tanks and abandon the
fence.

## Life inside the budget

Life belongs in the allocator because living systems receive ordered inputs,
perform work, store damage, recover, reproduce, and export consequences across
their boundaries. A living account measures retained capacity and imposed
degradation. It does not assign ownership of a person's total vitality to an
employer or state.

### Human perimeter

The body is the smallest protected perimeter. Occupational systems can account
for heat load, toxin exposure, injury, sleep disruption, and required recovery.
Individual medical data stays with the person, a clinic, or a union-controlled
trust. The employer receives a privacy-preserving workforce proof, usually at
cohort level, that its operation remained within voted exposure and recovery
limits.

A failed workforce proof opens a labor inquiry. It does not publish diagnoses.
Workers retain access to their own records and may carry a signed exposure proof
to another jurisdiction. A contract cannot waive the body's standing or roll a
worker's unused capacity into the employer's tradable surplus.

This closes the easiest accounting fraud. A factory cannot make its mechanical
dashboard green by replacing machinery with injured people. Manual cargo work,
night shifts, heat exposure, and recovery time consume the human component of
the perimeter profile.

### Ecological perimeter

Farms, fisheries, forests, and watersheds keep stock accounts for soil, water,
biomass, disease, and reproductive recovery. The budget follows the ecological
boundary when a property line cuts across it. A factory beside a river therefore
answers to its industrial perimeter and to the downstream watershed account.

Living systems may recover through growth. That recovery must be observed over
time before it becomes surplus. A replanted field does not erase topsoil loss on
the day seedlings appear.

### Houses and care

Threshold houses may publish their own life-inclusive proofs. Food yield, repair
capacity, soil condition, caregiver recovery, and resident health belong in the
same account. A house that survives through exhausted women, depleted ground, or
unrecorded outside supply has moved the burden out of sight. A sound house can
show where its replenishment came from and who had time to recover.

## The Bourse and bonded interconnects

Surplus is tradable within its class, geography, and time window. Thermal
capacity in one cooling basin cannot automatically move to another. Toxic
allowances remain compound-specific. Human recovery capacity is never traded by
an employer. Ecological credits require observed recovery and carry long
reversal periods.

Adjacent perimeters can transfer physical streams through a **bonded
interconnect**. The originating perimeter records custody transfer. The conduit
has its own seal and identity. The receiver accepts the liability. Both remain
answerable until the final treatment proof clears.

Several plants may register a shared district perimeter. Private contracts then
govern heat, exhaust, cooling water, and treatment inside the outer boundary.
The public sees the district's final crossings and stored liability. A neutral
trustee guild controls shared valves and resolves internal disputes.

A treatment core is called an **entropy sink** in market speech. It does not
destroy entropy. It converts a dangerous stream into a less harmful profile,
recovers useful material, or accepts a bonded stored liability. Clean-core
districts become valuable because they can perform those transformations
reliably.

Gradient passports travel with imported goods. A receiving perimeter can verify
embedded material depletion, unresolved toxic burden, labor-exposure compliance,
recoverability class, and ecological provenance without receiving the supplier's
process history. Goods without trusted provenance receive a conservative burden
or enter the grey market.

## Public governance

The allocator AI executes a published policy. It integrates measurements,
applies the current profile, reconciles stored liabilities, posts proofs, and
queues audits. It has no authority to decide which industries deserve priority.

Public votes and accountable technical bodies determine:

- budget priorities and emergency reserves;
- measurement boundaries and acceptable uncertainty;
- disclosure bands and privacy protections;
- sensor mandates, retrofit schedules, and appeal routes;
- transfer classes, geographic restrictions, and closure bonds;
- the model and certified implementation the allocator runs.

Measurements remain open to scientific challenge. Politics decides what burden
the community will accept and who receives scarce capacity. A public vote can
adopt a model or withdraw trust from one; it cannot change a thermometer's
reading.

Hospitals, water systems, food chains, and disaster response hold emergency
profiles with predeclared triggers. Emergency operation creates a public debt
and later review. It does not become a silent permanent exemption.

## Capture and perimeter warfare

Control over classification is control over productive life. An allocator can
use honest measurements while a captured government protects favored
perimeters, understates regional vulnerability, grants permanent emergency
status, or moves a boundary around the people paying the cost.

Common political attacks include:

- annexing worker housing into an industrial perimeter;
- splitting one dirty operator into nominally independent firms;
- routing an outlet across a weak jurisdiction;
- declaring a waste pond part of the private interior;
- assigning imported depletion to an unrecognized foreign perimeter;
- classifying a favored facility as essential after every emergency passes.

Perimeter sabotage becomes a form of war. An attacker can heat a dose capsule,
contaminate a chemical inlet, jam challenges, induce calibration drift, or dig a
false outlet that brings suspicion onto a rival. Capturing a Temple reference
standard can damage an entire region without destroying one factory.

## Black market — perimeter ghosting

**Perimeter ghosting** makes a boundary appear cleaner than it is. Its main
forms are:

| Fraud | Method | Detection pressure |
|---|---|---|
| **Shed spoofing** | Intercepts a sensor signal and lowers the sealed record | Random reference injection and independent logging |
| **Temporal laundering** | Releases waste during maintenance or network loss | Conservative missing-data estimates and physical dosimeters |
| **Physical bypass** | Uses a buried pipe, diffuse vent, or neighbor's property | Boundary sweeps, tracers, thermal imaging, subsurface surveys |
| **Buffer fraud** | Conceals stored liability or exaggerates recovery capacity | Inventory reconciliation and witnessed discharge tests |
| **Phantom surplus** | Claims useful recovery that never occurred | Mass balance, provenance checks, and peak-operation spot audits |
| **Perimeter arbitrage** | Moves a legal boundary around a burden | Public maps, affected-community standing, and joint liability |

Grey operators also trade clean cartridges, forged gradient passports, borrowed
trust streaks, and priority appointments with Marshals. Skilled audit attention
becomes a scarce commodity even when every instrument works correctly.

## Bloc implementations

### Continental Union

The Union keeps sophisticated stations and credible metrology while capturing
classification, emergency status, and resupply. Reserve perimeters receive broad
boundaries and permanent priority. Civilian districts inherit tight profiles,
slow appeals, and collective suspicion after a breach. The Fractured label can
move a body's costs into an institutional interior where the public ledger no
longer sees them.

UnionNet publishes clean proofs from a political map built to protect the cadre.
The corruption sits in whose perimeter counts, which regional baselines apply,
and who receives stamped replacement hardware.

### Eastern Mandate

The Mandate treats the perimeter as a technical ministry's operating unit.
Meridian supplies calibrated stations, gradient passports, and high-quality
models across member states. Local operators receive little power over model
choice or appeal timing. Replacement sensor cartridges and certified optical
elements become instruments of regional leverage.

Mandate districts excel at bonded industrial interconnects. Their weakness lies
in hidden worker draw and migrant dormitories folded into large production
perimeters. The measurements can remain precise while the boundary conceals who
absorbed the strain.

### Free Ports Coalition

The Ports inherit incompatible Union and Mandate hardware. Mechanics build
adapter manifolds, share reference gases, and rotate scarce certified parts.
PortsNet reconciles local proofs slowly; the dark mesh carries house audits and
unrecognized custody transfers ahead of formal recognition.

Free Port districts favor small nested perimeters and trustee guilds. Their
flexibility supports repair and shared treatment. It also permits quiet bypasses,
weak closure bonds, and reputation systems that fail when a respected operator
lies.

## Social and industrial consequences

- **Boundary law becomes constitutional law.** Surveyors, labor advocates,
  hydrologists, and metrologists contest the same maps.
- **Stored liability becomes finance.** Banks price corroding tanks, exhausted
  soil, workforce recovery debt, and uncertain provenance before lending.
- **Trust compounds.** Clean perimeters receive fewer audits and cheaper trades.
  Poor districts with old sensors enter a cycle of delay, suspicion, and lost
  capacity.
- **Repair changes design.** Modular machines, separable materials, exposed
  diagnostics, and standard fittings gain value because they reduce the boundary
  burden and stored liability.
- **Research needs failure allowances.** New processes begin without a trust
  streak. The Union reserves experimental profiles for the cadre, the Mandate
  credentials them, and the Ports improvise them.
- **Labor contracts become thermodynamic instruments.** Shift length, cooling,
  rest, transport, and recovery time change the living account.
- **Households inherit industrial cost.** A worker may leave a factory carrying
  fatigue or exposure that consumes care outside the fence. The living ledger
  makes that transfer contestable.
- **Ships become moving perimeters.** Hull identity, crew proofs, discharge
  fittings, cargo passports, and port reconciliation determine whether a vessel
  may dock.

## Consistency locks

- The perimeter is the regulatory unit. Individual machines may carry internal
  safeguards, but their private logs do not feed the state during ordinary
  compliance.
- The EEU profile remains multidimensional. No universal conversion makes every
  burden fungible.
- Stored waste carries forward and requires a closure bond.
- Physical interconnections cross boundaries only through bonded custody or a
  registered shared perimeter.
- ZKPs prove statements about certified readings. Drone calibration and human
  inspection establish whether those readings deserve trust.
- Life is included. Bodies keep sovereign standing and private records.
- The allocator executes public rules. It does not interpret people, determine
  civic worth, or choose political priorities.
- UnionNet, MandateNet, and PortsNet are the official meshes. The dark mesh is a
  separate fourth network.
- Original intent remains real: the system can measure consequential flows,
  protect privacy, and prevent genuine harm. Capture operates through boundary,
  priority, and classification.
- Traditional ownership continues for ordinary property. Title never grants
  ownership of another body, a commons, or immunity from perimeter liability.
|- EEU allocations are time-limited permissions. Stored liability follows a
|  regulated asset through sale, inheritance, restructuring, and salvage.
