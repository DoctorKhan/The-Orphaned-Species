# Two-Term Model — Book Instructions

> **Source lock:** `00_TWO_TERM_MODEL.md`
> **Status:** governing interpretation; overrides prose instincts that conflict.

---

## How to read this doc

This is a **structural overlay**, not a draft checklist. It maps every major missing beat from the cross-doc audit onto the Two-Term Model so you can see *why* each beat belongs and *what job* it is doing inside the spine. Use it when revising a chapter, planning a new scene, or testing whether a scene earns its place.

**Filter:**
- `[TTM]` = beat already expressible in current terms.
- `[TTM+structural]` = beat is missing and structurally required.
- `[TTM+policy]` = companion-layer matter; no draft obligation in the novel.

---

## The model in one page

| Term | What it is | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** | Community norm: shared practices, language, ideas of right/wrong | Provides local order; creates belonging; eventually fails |
| **T2** | Bridging mechanism: language + practice that can sit between any two endpoints | Negotiates translation indefinitely; does not resolve the gap, makes it productive |
| **Intermediate species** | The capacity created by T2 | A stratum between gods and animals; bridges across any culture, species, or plane |

**The series in one sentence:** T1 keeps failing; T2 keeps growing.

**The companion-layer jobs:**
- *The Social Game* diagnoses T1 as a system.
- *The Human Experiment* explains the origin of T2.
- *Manual Override* teaches T2 at personal scale.
- *The Cosmic Game* shows T2 at infinite-time / convergence horizon.

## Term 1 = the local attractor

**Drafting rule:** every group in the series must feel complete before it breaks.

**Books I–IV communities and their T1 shapes:**

| Community | Norm | Fatal gap |
|---|---|---|
| Pelangi Reach | Culture-first, negotiated, incomplete | The Reclamation's wealth dependency; the refugee who exposes the myth |
| Singapore | Registry, order, evidence-based control | Cannot account for what it has not measured; Eli's receiver |
| Malacca cooperative | Live fields, Maren's bounded journal, Anika's offer | War pressure + Rasel's coerced physics; cannot hold the civilian gate |
| Albion cloister | Men's cloister, repair crews, veterans, thread elders | Post-release relief that cannot fund itself; Somchai's crown temptation |
| Brazilian river settlements | River-port network, clinics, growers, threshold houses | Continental fracture; Lang's persuasive offer when welcome fails |
| Hawaiʻi compact | Eagle–Condor teaching, Awakening War | Forced synchronization; the compact tested under war pressure |
| North American court | Women-led court, Costa Rican hinge, regional games | Lean Year collapse; anocratic remnants vs. hemispheric entitlement |

**Prose test:** if a scene shows only the norm and no pressure, it is exposition. Add the refugee, the artifact, the refusal, the failure.

## Term 2 = the bridging mechanism

**Drafting rule:** every volume must show T2 emerging from the wreckage of T1.

**Book-by-book T2 beats:**

### Book I — The Breach

| T1 norm | Gap | T2 mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Made home at Pelangi | Refugee with dangerous receiver exposed to global fight | Eli's receiver: he connects the unrelated; pattern-leaps that look like magic, test like science |
| Malacca cooperative | War pressure + coerced physics | Anika's true offer + civilian gate (Wren uses real name and house standing) |
| Thailand / Sanxingdui | Fragmentary field data; nobody has the whole picture | Maren's distributed Tree of Life: the living world connected to itself |

**Key scene filter:** does this beat give the reader a *practice* or *form* that survives the breakdown of the local norm? The breach activates Eli's receiver; the civilian gate redistributes evacuation authority; Sanxingdui recognizes distributed connection. All three are T2.

### Book III — The Living World [TTM+structural]

These beats are structurally required and currently missing from the draft.

| T1 norm | Gap | T2 mechanism | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eagle–Condor compact | Teaching contested rather than fulfilled; cooperation without merger | A bounded civic form that can hold difference without becoming a state | Brazil → Hawaiʻi leg; federal compact scene |
| Two-Tree reciprocal-circuit civic procedure | Knowledge and Life keep splitting into rival authorities | Three accounts per decision: embodied, technical, relational; Life ↔ Knowledge ↔ people check one another | Federal compact scene; court of threads preview |
| Drift / god-sickness / synchronization refusal | Forced sync offers healing, belonging, relief — it is locally attractive | Refusal without shame; the healer who can say no; Eli refusing Sol-as-Jamuka | Drift scene; Somchai precedent |
| Complex-time research sequence | Time as prison, past/future as fixed | Research sequence showing lateral / adjacent states as operational | Field office / Commissioner Ma / Mandate industry leg |
| Sol-as-Jamuka forced-unity arc | The attractive ruler who promises to end fracture by ending difference | Force-unity fails because it abolishes the gap that T2 needs | Hawaiʻi climax; Sol's offer and refusal |
| Temple / oath / armor / coda | The civic victory needs a visible form | Teahouse pilgrimage sites; portable disciplines; service houses; shared meals; work-songs | Post-compact settlement; coda beat |
| Back-cover birds / morning landing | Relief that could become complacency | Ordinary life with wisdom, now consciously chosen | Closing image; Eli/Wren domestic scene |

**Double-Fork Stone / dragon ghost [TTM+structural]:** a contested object whose interpretation divides the cast. One side reads it as furnace-making evidence; the other as absence. The object carries no unique absence evidence itself — the dispute is the point. It dramatizes T1/T2: the community's norm produces two rival readings; the bridging mechanism (witnessed attestation, independent verification, right of refusal) does not resolve the dispute but makes it productive. Draft it as a scene where the disagreement itself *is* the civic procedure.

**Named male battlefield death [TTM+structural]:** required to test whether the compact can absorb cost without reverting to revenge. Not an extra; it is the T2 mechanism under pressure: does the community's bridging practice hold when one of its own dies?

**Threshold-house loss [TTM+structural]:** a house or clinic destroyed. Tests whether the compact can rebuild *differently* rather than simply restoring the old form. Loss → redesign is the T2 beat; loss → restoration is T1 reasserting.

## Term 2 at character scale

**Eli:** his arc is becoming the person who can *hold the gap* — receive contact, clarify the web, refuse ownership. T2 made flesh.

**Wren / Rowan:** the Weavers weave the *human* net that T2 needs to survive. Houses, routes, hands, memory. The human infrastructure without which the bridging mechanism has no carrier.

**Somchai:** the body-scale T2. He holds a real gift and refuses ownership of it. The cost is real: someone suffers because he won't be their miracle. That cost is the proof.

**Rasel:** T2 at father scale. Symbolic feeling without command; the garden as the thing he tends badly and honestly.

**Maren:** T2 at release scale. The taut thread going slack. The amplifier runs on wanting; release is the one input it cannot metabolize.

## Term 2 and the intermediate species

The "intermediate species" is not a character. It is the *capacity* that T2 creates: the ability to sit between gods and animals, between cultures, between planes, without being consumed by either.

**Where this appears on-page:**

- **Books I–II:** implicit. Eli lives inside the gap; he does not name it.
- **Book III:** partially named. The federal compact, the reciprocal circuit, the compact without merger.
- **Book IV:** the court of threads tests whether T2 can govern without becoming a new T1.
- **Books V–X:** T2 becomes ambient culture. Teahouses, cloisters, flights, braids. The intermediate species is now the *default* mode of being — and the story asks what happens when the bridging mechanism tries to become a first-order attractor itself. That is the angelic-orders tension deferred to V+.

## Scene filter for revision

**For every scene in the draft, ask:**

1. Does this scene show a T1 norm that feels complete?
2. Does a pressure arrive that the norm cannot metabolize?
3. Does a T2 practice emerge — or fail to emerge — from the wreckage?

If the answer to 1 is yes and 2 is no: the scene is exposition. Add the pressure.
If the answer to 1 is no and 2 is yes: the scene is abstract. Ground it in a specific community's norm.
If the answer to 3 is no: the scene is a missed beat. Add the practice or show its failure.

**One-line filter for any chapter:**

> *What is the community's norm (T1), what breaks it, and what bridging practice (T2) rises — or fails to rise — in the gap?*
