## Reference frame

- stakes species-level; **body stays ridiculous**;
- smart and **undignified** at once;
- physical comedy **during** problem-solving;
- buddy warmth between scared people doing careful work;
- kinetic rhythm, then one quiet beat.

**Not:** cosmology parody, quip-bro defusal, comic relief who dies first, jokes at mother-release climax.

## Distribution — LOCKED 2026-08-01 (resolves Emrys purge gap)

No single primary carrier. The full instrument-slapstick register (earnest over-explanation, undignified competence, buddy panic, wrong register at wrong time, measuring when he should touch) was built for one mentor archetype that no longer exists in the cast, and forcing all of it onto an existing character flattens someone who's doing real work elsewhere — Rasel's established love/anger/compromise, Wren's armor-break, Somchai's grief-driven vocation. Comedy distributes instead: the ensemble register already below stays as the main engine, and two existing characters take one narrow, bounded slice each — only the piece that's genuinely true to them, not the whole register on loan.

- **Rasel — narrow, post-rescue only (Book I close / Book II open).** Not goofball; one specific crack in his competence-armor: he processes love through precision because he doesn't have a more direct language for it — over-explaining coupling-instrument specs or garden chemistry at the exact moment Eli needs something less technical. Poignant-funny, a handful of beats, never an engine. Already seeded (`31` Ch 13's tomato-tied-to-lab-cuff ending image; his "chosen craft Eli cannot master").
- **Somchai — gallows/practical humor, his own scenes only (Book II).** Dark, dry, competence-adjacent field-medic humor — a real coping register for someone who does triage — distinct from the Vol I–II fellowship register below. Supports his established vulnerability-under-competence arc; never undercuts the "son of a man" gravity of his central beat.

The buddy-warmth and physical-comedy-during-problem-solving DNA that made the old register work survives through **whoever's in the room under pressure** — Eli, Wren, and situational local experts working a problem together — rather than one character performing comic relief.
