# Comedic Register — Slapstick Under Catastrophe

> **Status:** working craft guide. **Project Hail Mary** (2026) comedy grammar — goofball competence, physical gags, earnest panic while solving impossible problems, buddy warmth — without IP import. Primary carrier: **Emrys**. Pair with `26_present_day_cast_expanded.md`, `27_character_development_arcs.md` (Emrys arc), `28_present_day_trilogy_craft_index.md`, `05_story_architecture.md` § Manuscript Voice, `16_reader_attachment.md`.

## Reference frame

- stakes species-level; **body stays ridiculous**;
- smart and **undignified** at once;
- physical comedy **during** problem-solving;
- buddy warmth between scared people doing careful work;
- kinetic rhythm, then one quiet beat.

**Not:** cosmology parody, quip-bro defusal, comic relief who dies first, jokes at mother-release climax.
