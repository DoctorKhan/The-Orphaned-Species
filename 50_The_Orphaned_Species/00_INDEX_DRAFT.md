## How to read this

- **LIVE** — current canon; draft or revise against this.
- **QUARRY** — superseded but preserved for salvageable prose/ideas; do not draft from it as current plot.
- **PROCESS** — working scratch (open questions, backlogs, decision logs); resolve and archive, don't treat as canon.
- **Collision** — two files currently share a number prefix; flagged for renumbering, not yet fixed.
