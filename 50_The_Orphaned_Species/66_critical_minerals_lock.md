# Critical Minerals Lock

> Status: governing lock, 2026-08-10.

This note locks critical minerals into the civic frame. Earlier research substrate stays
in `WORLD_BIBLE.md` § *Timeline* and `50_The_Orphaned_Species/59_younger_dryas_nucleation_lock.md`.
New prose should pull from here, not invent from the raw briefing.

## Governing constraint

The 2025-2026 pattern is export control and mid-stream concentration, not only
territorial ownership. A bloc that dominates refining controls the current that
actually passes through manufacture. Raw deposit without processing capacity is
geopolitical theater.

## Locked moves

1. **Refining is the chokepoint.** Mining output matters less than electrolytic capacity,
metal separation, magnet manufacture, and component assembly. The controlling regime
owns the node between earth and usable hardware.

2. **AI demand and decarbonization demand run in the same direction.** Data centers,
EV platforms, grid hardware, and defense systems all need the same narrow chemical
families. A supply disruption forces allocation choices; the chooser sets national
priority.

3. **Export controls replaced tariffs as the first weapon.** Policy choices now target
specific chemistries and downstream components, not just ores. A single published
directive can freeze logistics across multiple sectors at once.

4. **Downstream control outranks upstream ownership.** Nations that refine, machine,
and recycle keep leverage even when they import almost every feed material.

5. **Recycling and substitution are strategies, not backups.** Urban mining of
discarded hardware and alternative chemistries are treated as strategic capacity.
Old data centers, turbine blades, and vehicle packs become stockpile language.

6. **Traceability becomes entry policy.** Alliances are moving to mineral gradient
passports. Origin matters at the ore, concentrate, metal, component, and final-product
stages. This raises documentation cost and makes forgery a first-class industrial risk.

7. **Africa and Latin America demand equity at the plant gate.** Resource states are
moving past raw export toward local processing, technology transfer, and domestic
refining. The question is no longer who owns the ground but who owns the registered
boundary where value concentrates.

## Craft use in novel prose

Do not teach the reader this as policy literature. Plant it as:

- delivery deadline and a denied component order;
- refinery manifest and an unexplained hold;
- two characters reading different risk from the same port notice;
- the same depot serving three incompatible traceability systems;
- a youth practice that treats dead battery packs as inventory, not trash.

Term budget: one new chemistry per chapter at most.
Deep-time residue: one reference per one or two chapters.
Desire before info; effect before terminology; one supply-chain reveal per chapter.
