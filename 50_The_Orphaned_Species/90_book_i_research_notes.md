## Chapter One — somatic interoception

Ordinary nerves read heat, pressure, and paresthesia from body and near-body states.
Real anchor: A. D. Craig's interoception model ("How Do You Feel? An Interoceptive
Moment with Your Neurobiological Self," *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*, 2009) names
the actual pathway — fine-grained internal-state signaling that ordinary bodies
already do. Eli's **somatic signatures**—the mother's diagnostic read through
palms—are **in-fiction amplification** of that real pathway, not a verified
clinical phenotype.

## Chapter Three — hypoxia, grounding, and the Circles

- **Hypoxia and sensory flooding.** Transient global hypoxia can acutely alter
  thalamocortical gating. Two real, checkable anchors sharpen this beyond generic
  "contested" hand-waving: Borjigin et al. (*PNAS*, 2013) found a transient surge
  of highly synchronized gamma-band activity in the seconds after cardiac arrest
  in rats, proposed as a possible correlate of vivid near-death consciousness;
  Timmermann et al. (*Frontiers in Psychology*, 2018) found that controlled DMT
  dosing produces experience-reports that resemble published near-death-experience
  reports more closely than other altered states. Together they give the Malacca
  breach a real floor and a real bridge to the puberty DMT trips — both converge
  on the same neurophenomenological territory (`90_epigraphs_and_sources.md` §5D).
  Eli's opened receiver remains **in-fiction amplification** on top of that floor.
  **Malacca = activation without training**—receiver on, not skill or curriculum.
- **Grounding.** Temperature-focus, breath-counting, and single-object attention are
  standard somatic de-escalation techniques in trauma care. Keeper practice extends
  real technique; it does not replace medicine.
- **The Circles.** The Three-Circles Tablet is a fictional/disputed story artifact
  (`35`), not a known excavated object. It is a Body / Family / Civilization
  diagnostic only; Eli cannot yet read the outer rings.

## Chapter Four — pursuit craft

- **Auditory misdirection.** Trained listeners orient to salient acoustic patterns;
  masking and decoy signals are standard countermeasures. The sonic misroute extends
  real practice into the story's psionic register.
- **Coastal smuggling economies.** Manifest-slot allocation, seasonal passes, and
  kin-based crew substitution reflect documented practices in Southeast Asian
  coastal trades; the specific institutions are fictional composites.

## Chapter Five — Thailand route and practice

- **Mountain routing under pursuit.** High, low-surveillance roads can evade
  attention concentrated on ports and valley corridors. Khun Dang's lodge and the
  Mae Hong Son loop are fictional composites pending research. Pai keeps the hairpin
  stair and town carnival; the coffee-farm spur is Chiang Dao–coded without naming
  Chiang Dao on the Pai road.
- **Silent retreat practice.** The wat sequence draws on Thai monastic routines—
  anapanasati, walking meditation (*cankama*), mindful eating, body scanning, and
  metta—encountered as ordinary discipline, not psion training.
- **Plants as instrumentation.** Vegetation responds measurably to soil
  conductivity, moisture gradients, and subsurface structure; crop marks over
  buried archaeology are a standard survey phenomenon. The forming line extends
  that detector logic into the story's register.

## Chapter Six — traditional birth attendants

Palpation-based fetal positioning, fever management, and the social peril of
women's medical knowledge in patriarchal settings are documented across South
Asian histories of midwifery. Ila's diagnostic touch is the story's amplification
of real, practiced, dangerous expertise.

## Chapter Seven — field method

## Chapter Eight — bleeding and metabolic cost

- **Femoral pressure points.** External compression proximal to a junctional bleed
  is standard first aid; locating the point quickly in poor light is difficult and
  decisive. Eli's contribution is location under panic—perception, not repair.
- **Post-exertional crash.** Tremor, tunnel vision, and compulsive hunger are the
  story's fixed metabolic price for reception.

## Chapter Nine — crowd dynamics

Panic propagation through crowds follows measurable herd dynamics. The driven panic
extends real crowd physics into the story's register: an attack on collective
attention, not a mystery ray. Drownings, crush injuries, and fire remain ordinary
material consequences.

## Chapter Ten — redundant records

The surviving carbon copy, wall safe, and duplicate samples follow the data-integrity
principle of keeping copies in different failure domains. The cooperative's habits,
not Eli's abilities, preserve the next clue.
