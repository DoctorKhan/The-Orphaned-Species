## One cycle

These schools are not competing answers. They are **passes** through one
motion:

```text
sensation only
    → story-I (identification)
        → dissolve (anatta / Advaita / sense-absorption / being-rest)
            → return
                → usable ego, cleaner relation
                    → more engagement from one level up (soul / Player)
```

The series' distinctive claim is the **return**. Dissolution without return
is the observer trap, Drift, and the deathless loop. Reformation is not
failure. It is how the soul can engage.

---

## Two facts about the "I"

**1. In pure observation there is no self, only sensation.**
The Buddhists are correct at this pass. No owner in the raw data. Heat,
pressure, light, sound. The movie with no one in the seat yet.

**2. Identification is a second step.**
A story that there is an *I*, and that the sensation is happening *to* it.
Like watching a film and then putting yourself in the frame. Superficial:
it is added, not found. The false I is imagined.

These two facts do not abolish agency. They locate it. The Player is not
the imagined I. The imagined I is a costume the machine puts on so
autopilot can run a character.

**Do not collapse the chain.** These are not synonyms and not rungs of
altitude.

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Sensation** | Raw data. Heat, pressure, light, sound. No owner in it. You do not "fuse" with heat. Heat is already there. |
| **Charge** | Impulse deposited; guidance relating to the agency-place (`68`). Push, pull, or continue. |
| **Emotion** | The machine's **response to charge**. Fear, anger, sweetness as a reaction — not the charge itself. |
| **Identification** | The second-step story: there is an I, and this is happening to it. Superficial. Added. |

Identification can dress an emotion ("I am the one who is afraid") or a
thought. It is not a merger with sensation. Sense-absorption is resting
*as* observation of sensation so that second step does not fire — not
becoming the heat.

## Two dissolvers

Both **disintegrate** the story-I. Neither is the home.

| Dissolver | What you do | Tradition-face | Risk if you stay |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sense-absorption** | Rest in sensation until the owner-story cannot form | Buddhist sati at the body; what some call tantra | Pleasure-chase; treating contact as the destination (Life Drift) |
| **Being-rest** | Look for the self and find nothing | Advaita; anatta as inquiry | Sterile witness, spiritual ego ("I am awareness"), non-entropic loop |

**Do not call the first tantra on-page** unless a specific lineage earns the
word. Author-side: **sense-absorption** (Tree of Life dissolving the story
downward into contact). The second: **being-rest** (look, find none).
Together: **the two dissolvers**.

Coming back from Advaita's nothing is what makes ego-*formation* visible.
You cannot see how the costume is put on until you have watched it fall off.

## Two builders (after the dissolve)

**Anatta** (as practice, not only as fact) combats the *formation* of the
false I — the imagined owner. It is a guard at the second step.

**The chair and the wound — LOCKED 2026-08-16.** A working pass for that
guard. Sit with an object you are not identified with — a chair, a
floorboard, a post. Sensation only: grain, weight, light. No owner in it.
When that look is stable, let the mind move to something that *has* an I
— the sternum after Mei's dinner, a father's ask, the heir-fear. Then
back to the chair. Then the wound. Keep going until the same look holds
on both.

The middle is not a compromise between the two objects. It is the
relation that was easy on the chair becoming available on the wound.
Stay only on the chair and you are hiding (observer trap). Stay only on
the wound and the second step is running you. The oscillation is how
anatta stops being a fact about furniture and becomes a guard at
identification.

I–IV may do this as a wat drill (post, then heat, then post) without the
word. Named anatta and the two-object pass as liturgy wait for V–X (`68`).

**Sati** adds a **real self–object relationship**, but **upward in altitude**.
Not a thicker story-I. A cleaner pointer: this sensation, this object, this
choice — related, not fused, not denied. That is the return that enables
engagement. The chair-and-wound pass is how you *find* that pointer.

Reformation of the ego after either dissolver **clarifies the relationship**
and increases engagement from the soul, one level up. The robot becomes
usable. Correct worship: robot submits to Player (`SERIES_STRUCTURE`).
