## How to read this

- **LIVE** — current canon; draft or revise against this.
- **QUARRY** — superseded; salvage prose/ideas only; do not draft plot from it.
- **PROCESS** — working scratch. Do not treat as canon.
- **Collision** — two files share a number prefix; flagged, not yet renamed.

## Propagation rule — LOCKED 2026-08-16

A lock is not done when only the bible moved.

- If the lock changes a **scene** (what happens, who is in the room, what the reader must feel), the same pass names the manuscript chapter and edits it, or names the chapter as an open prose debt in `100_production_todo.md`.
- If the lock changes only a **class-word** or author-side mapping (Elohim, Player liturgy, V–X self-formation), the pass names `45` / `95` / `62` and stops. Do not dump it into I–IV prose.
