A place is a plot engine. It arrives through soles, breath, stomach, a wrong turn.

**Five moves (every major location):**

1. **Signature geometry** — one body-memorable shape. Return visits reuse the same knees, breath, or sound.
2. **Class and truth as altitude** — up/down (surface/Thread, bund/dead field, tourist path/sealed door) is who eats, who knows, who is watched. Put Eli's body through the grade change.
3. **Lived systems first** — tongs, wage tickets, gantries, sluices, backfill clocks, before what the place claims to mean.
4. **Wonder and dread in the same room** — wonder before ambush (`55` §8).
5. **Revisit changed** — Pelangi return (Vol III); Singapore as memory; Sanxingdui after Eli leaves Rasel.

**Each major location needs all four:**

| # | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Wonder breath** — one held moment of awe before violence or a thesis |
| 2 | **Signature sensation** — sound, smell, pressure, or floor-hum unique to that place |
| 3 | **Ritual or machine that runs without Eli** — labor someone else is already doing |
| 4 | **Place lie** — what the place claims vs what the scene shows |

Do not name *Silo* on-page. Stage the geometry: spiral stair, sealed atrium, class as altitude.
