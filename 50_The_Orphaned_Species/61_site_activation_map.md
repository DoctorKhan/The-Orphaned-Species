## What this solves

The bible and timeline are too dense to dump into chapters. Instead:

**Every major timeline fact becomes a discovery at a charged site** — an object,
practice, archive haul, dream-residue, or living witness — that **activates**
under present pressure and changes the next move.

You do not “cover Phase VI.” You arrive at a place, something wakes, someone
argues about what it means, and the route or relationship costs something.

---

## The activation recipe (use every time)

Fill one card per beat before expanding prose:

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| **Site** | Where are they standing? (touristable / body-memorable) |
| **Pressure** | What clock forces them here *now*? (rescue / registry / civilian / Sol) |
| **Carrier** | Object, practice, scan, song, living person, or dream? |
| **Channel** | Material / human / linguistic / dream-reception (`35` transmission rule) |
| **Bible bite** | One timeline or companion fact — *one*, not a phase dump |
| **Wrong first** | What do they think it means? (must be usable and false) |
| **Partial truth** | What do they earn that changes tomorrow? |
| **Deep-lore hold** | What stays *Elmarillion*/reader-only until a later book? |
| **Desire cost** | Which want gets worse? (home / parents / Wren / belonging / control) |
| **Next door** | Where does this force them next? |

**Hard rules**

1. **Effects before causes** (`17`). Feel it → argue → name later.
2. **One new named concept per chapter** (`55` item 15).
3. **Payoff in 1–3 chapters** — not “wait for Book IV.”
4. **Finding ≠ full immersion.** Dream/descent only when site + pressure + route change demand it (`35`, `36` §8).
5. **Rootbook is matrix, not master key** (`52`). It opens a door; the site corroborates or corrects.

## One-paragraph drafting loop

1. Open the chapter’s beatsheet row.  
2. Pick **one** bible bite from this map (or the pull list).  
3. Fill the recipe card.  
4. Write the scene so the **carrier** is visible and the **wrong first** is spoken by someone on-page.  
5. End on the **next door** and a **desire cost**.  
6. If you needed a second new term, cut one (`55` §15).
