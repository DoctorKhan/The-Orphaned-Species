# Demographic Handoff — The Orphaned Species

## What is already locked
- Three male fates: **House men**, **Thread men**, **Stray men**.
- Stray men have no lane, no standing, no registry slot.
- Caught Stray man consequences by zone: exile / criminal charge in Mandate; transfer to Continental Union penal system; in free harbors, tolerated only as disposable labor (bodyguard, security, hard manual work, high-risk jobs).
- Core Stray-man rule: invisibility.

## Baseline shock: COVID
- COVID preferentially killed males at approximately **1.75 males to 1 female**.
- This is the *starting demographic* for all post-COVID zone calculations.
- Every zone's current ratio must be computed from this depleted male baseline, then layered with policy and migration.

## Key distinction
- **General population ratio** = all residents, including House men, Thread men, Stray men, women, children.
- **Stray-man ratio** = only men in the Stray condition. This is effectively **100% male** by definition.
- Do not conflate "zone has more males" with "zone has more Stray men." They are separate questions.

## Provisional zone ratios (general population)

### Eastern Mandate
- **Official policy target:** 60 female : 40 male (patriarchal control).
- **Actual street ratio:** likely near even or slightly female-skewed, e.g., 52/48 to 55/45, because COVID depleted males below what policy aimed for.
- **Stray-man fraction:** low. Mandate crushes or absorbs unregulated men. Stray men who exist here are hard to see, not numerous.

### Continental Union
- **General population:** likely reflects COVID baseline, maybe 55/45 to 60/40 female/male.
- **Penal/inmate population:** heavily male. The Union imports men for penal labor, so the *institutional* ratio is skewed male even if the general population is not.
- **Stray-man fraction:** many Stray men end up here, but they lose the label and become convict labor.

### Free harbors
- **General population:** near 50/50. Transit/mercantile churn flattens local skew.
- **Stray-man fraction:** visible because they are the disposable labor layer, but transient. They do not settle; they pass through, take dangerous jobs, move on, or get caught and sent to Union.
- **Pressure:** not population density, but legal exposure. Too many visible Stray men breaks the tacit tolerance.
