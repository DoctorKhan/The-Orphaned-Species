## Operating principle — LOCKED

Timah and Jul are working people with a documented, inherited, trained autonomic adaptation. Their skills are mundane biology used operationally: dive reflex, breath discipline, heart-rate control, and local underwater route knowledge. They are not latent carriers, not psionic, not stealth tech, and not instruments.

Their narrative function is the same in every book: **the protagonists face a route problem that no surface credential, legal channel, or surface vessel can solve. Timah/Jul supply the one unmapped local detail that makes an alternative leg possible. They do not perform the rescue. They do not command the corridor. They are not rewarded with standing. They are paid, or owed, or both.**

This file pins one locked appearance per remaining book. Where no other character can replicate their specific skills, they appear. Where a surface solution exists, they stay on the fringe.

## Book II — *The Descent*: Ch 5 Bosporus–Thrace/Balkan holding corridor

**Locked slot:** Ch 5 — *What They Took* (Thrace/Balkan holding site), westbound transfer corridor from the Bosporus.

**Plot problem:** Hassan is moving under medical-transfer cover. Custodian and registry surface crossings are either watched or burned. Wren's corridor record is a paper life; it cannot show depth, current, or submerged structure. Rowan's keeper line can open one crossing only by burning another. The group needs a lane that the Custodian file never marked because the route does not exist on the surface.

**Timah/Jul function:** They know the strait gap's submerged channel from harvesting: a submerged shelf step, a current that carries debris away from a sensor blind angle, and a depth that keeps a hull draft from fouling. The detail is small, physical, and unverifiable from any registry chart. It lets Wren/Rowan position the crossing where the transfer corridor cannot see it. Timah keeps the household out of the sampling map by refusing any public credit; Jul observes whether he wants to stay hidden or help again.

**Non-negotiable guardrails:**
- Hassan still reaches the holding corridor because of Wren/Rowan's paper work and keeper authority. Timah/Jul make the timing legible, not possible.
- No surface chase converts this into an action showcase for them.
- Their payment is ordinary: fuel, a quiet favor, or the promise that nobody traces the detail back to their street.

**Beat effect:** Eli's mother-hunt continues; this is logistics, not rescue. Their presence seeds the coast/river work that pays off in Book III.

## Book III — *The Compact*: Ch 1–2 Brazilian lower-river harbor intake and coastal maneuver

**Locked slot:** Ch 1–2 — *The Boat at Morning* / *Terms of Welcome* (Brazilian lower-river receiving harbor and river-port network), coastal approach after the Atlantic crossing.

**Plot problem:** Newly awakened refugees arrive by small boat under pursuit. Surface port authority, registry intake, and coastal survey all operate against them. Mandate/Continuity pressure means the visible harbor is a classification trap. Eli's first coordination beat is crowd control inside the port; he cannot simultaneously pilot or reroute outside. The coast-watcher and island-approach beats already locked for Book III need a local lane that official charts omit.

**Timah/Jul function:** They run the submerged channel into the receiving harbor—a route below the intake sensors and below the surface wake the Mandate monitors. Their breath-hold and heart-rate discipline let them move in water cold enough to suppress the thermal signature standard port scanners track. Jul carries a small packet or leads a boat through the blind; Timah reads the water and withdraws. They are not the crowd's coordinators. They are the reason the boat is inside the perimeter before anyone knows to look.

**Non-negotiable guardrails:**
- Eli's crowd redistribution still earns the Ch 1 victory. Timah/Jul are the door, not the organizer.
- No tactical exposition frames their skills as "stealth" or "cloaking." Their adaptation is ordinary biology, applied.
- The scene does not end with gratitude speech. It ends with them already back on the fringe, unpaid and uncredited, because that is how the household survives.

**Beat effect:** This is the speed win in coastal channels the Mandate didn't map. It also gives Eli the first material example of what Timah/Jul can do, without commentary.

## Book IV — *The Court of Threads*: Ch 5–7 Hollow Year coastal corridor and convoy defense

**Locked slot:** Ch 5–7 — post-eruption coastal corridor and convoy-defense stretch, after eruption damage closes or seizes official routes and before the court stabilizes allocation.

**Plot problem:** The Hollow Year seizes interregional routes. Official convoy channels are seized, hoarded, or rerouted by Continuity and private security. Coastal and island ports remain live, but the waterways between them are partially blocked, partially surveilled, and partially abandoned. Eli's compact needs one corridor that moves food, medicine, or people across water the court's ledger has already written off.

**Timah/Jul function:** They know the abandoned coastal lane from years of harvesting in exactly the kind of marginal water the eruption has now made critical: a submerged river mouth, a tidal channel, a kelp-shadowed pass that carries no surface trace and no registry record. Their breath-hold and bradycardia response let them move submerged long enough to verify depth, mark a safe line, or guide a small hull through. They are the logistics pair who turn a written-off corridor into a working route for one convoy.

**Non-negotiable guardrails:**
- They do not fight. They do not command. They do not become Eli's commanders or trusted lieutenants.
- The convoy still succeeds because of Eli's compact structure and named local crews, not because of their heroism.
- Their return is silent: they keep the household, keep the fringe, and keep the court from knowing the route exists.

**Beat effect:** This is the Hollow Year's modular interdependence made concrete: local knowledge plus distributed compact plus unmapped water. The book's test is whether institutions can hold without Eli; this scene proves they can also move without him.

## Character continuity

**Timah:** Her elder authority is harbor infrastructure. She will help if the cost to her household stays low and the credit stays off the street. Her leverage is Jul and her memory predates registries. In every book she says less than she knows and keeps the detail physical.

**Jul:** He is still deciding whether staying hidden is enough. Book II shows him watching; Book III shows him acting; Book IV shows him choosing the route without waiting for Timah's say-so. His arc is not rebellion. It is whether useful work and safety can coexist.

---

## Prose guardrails

- No "not X but Y" framing around their skills. They are not mystical, not latent carriers, not instruments.
- No exposition naming bradycardia, spleen size, or vasoconstriction. Show it through behavior: slow surface pulse, cold hands, long submersion, ordinary comment.
- No scene in which a protagonist thanks them as a savior. At most a quiet exchange of fuel, food, or kept silence.
- If a scene requires them to perform rescue, it fails the lock. Return it to the drawing board.
