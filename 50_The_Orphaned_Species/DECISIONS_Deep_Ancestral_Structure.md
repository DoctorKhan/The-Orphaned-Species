## 2. The science, in plain claims

Three models are reading the same Pleistocene DNA. They disagree on the *shape* of 1.5 mya–300 kya. Do not stack them as one event.

### 2.1 Cousins, Scally, Durbin 2025 (*Nature Genetics*) — `cobraa`

A coalescent HMM on 1000 Genomes / HGDP diploids.

- One ancestral population splits ~**1.5 mya** into **A** and **B**.
- They stay mostly apart until ~**290–300 kya**.
- They rejoin ~**80% A / 20% B**. That mix is in **every living human**.
- A has a **sharp bottleneck right after the 1.5 mya split**, then grows until the rejoin.
- B is inferred as **larger and more stable**. B’s size was held constant in the model because it could not be resolved. Soft number.
- B-derived sequence is **stripped near genes** (bad on an A body) and **enriched in some neuronal categories**.
- Where a living genome looks more like A, it is closer to Neanderthal and Denisovan sequence. So **N and D come from A**. They do **not** carry the 20% B.

`cobraa` sees **sequence ancestry**. It does not see chromosome count, fusion scars, fossils, or a named species. “Ghost” here means: no labelled skeleton and no sequenced B individual.

### 2.2 Hu et al. 2023 (*Science*) — the bottleneck you already use

Assumes one mixed population. Infers a crash to ~**1,280** breeders from **~930–813 kya**.

This is the number under Phase II and `91` §2.1. Other methods do not recover that crash. A 2025 critique argues a structured history can look like a bottleneck if you assume panmixia, and that Cousins’ A-bottleneck at 1.5 mya is **not the same event** as Hu’s 117,000-year crash.

**Honesty line:** keep the fusion date (Poszewiecka 2022: 0.9 mya, CI 0.4–1.5). Treat Hu’s census as one reading of a disputed signal. Cousins is the competing structured-population reading.

### 2.3 Ragsdale et al. 2023 (*Nature*) — leaky African stems

Two African stems split ~1–1.7 mya and keep leaking until ~500 kya. Mixing is a long drip. There is no single 20% pulse at 300 kya.

Hold Ragsdale vs Cousins as **unsettled**. A 2040 scientist in-story would not have picked a winner. The cave may prefer Cousins because it gives a harvestable A and a sister B.

### 2.4 Zhang, Biddanda, Moorjani et al. 2026 (*Science*) — TRACE

Ordinary small introgression. Different paper from Cousins.

- ~**0.5–1%** in every living human from a lineage that split ~**800 kya** (same radiation window as N/D) and mixed in Africa before the last Out-of-Africa.
- A ~**1.8 mya** “super-archaic” line mixes into Denisovans more than 200 kya; a sliver reaches some modern people, especially Oceania, through later Denisovan admixture.
- Together with known N/D DNA, extra archaic sequence sits around **~2%**, not 20%.
- Those segments lean toward immunity and metabolism.

Do not collapse TRACE into Cousins. TRACE is a fourth sibling in the Great Divergence plus older dirt on the Denisovan branch.

| Event | Current best science | Your file |
|---|---|---|
| Sapiens / N+D split | ~800–550 kya | Great Divergence ~800–400 kya |
| N vs D split | ~470–380 kya | same window |

| Denisovan → some *sapiens* | after that shared N pulse | same |

Cousins does **not** redate N or D. It only says they are children of A and miss B.

The stale date is the **70 kya mix**. The expansion can still start ~70–60 kya. The Neanderthal pulse itself has moved to ~50–45 kya. `91` already flags Reich’s cultural-innovation reading of the expansion.

### 2.6 Chromosome 2 fusion (separate literature)

IJdo et al. 1991; Poszewiecka et al. 2022: fusion at **0.9 mya (CI 0.4–1.5)**. Living humans, Neanderthals, and Denisovans all carry it.

Because N and D come from A and leave A before 300 kya, **A was already fused** before they split off.

**B’s chromosome count is unknown.** B left no genome. B’s letters now sit on our 46 chromosomes, including on fused chromosome 2. After a 46×48 cross, recombination can copy B sequence onto A’s fused chromosome. The scar we carry is A’s scar.

A 20% genome-wide pulse is easier if A and B were both 46 when they joined. A 46×48 mismatch can still leak DNA. Heterozygotes have reduced fertility (unbalanced gametes). They are not sterile. Underdominance then makes one count win. We are 46. If B was 48, A’s count won. What can survive from B is sequence, not B’s chromosome number.

The oddity is the **size**. Neanderthals are closer kin, share the fusion, and left ~2%. B had been apart since 1.5 mya and still left ~20%, then was selected against near genes. That wants an extra cause: B was also 46, or B was huge, or the contact was dense, or something favored the mixed children.

### 2.7 “No bones”

Overstated. Africa in that window is full of bones (*erectus*, *heidelbergensis* / *rhodesiensis*, Jebel Irhoud, Florisbad). Cousins does not attach B to any of them. Ancient DNA almost never survives African heat at that age. “No genome” is what Africa does to DNA. “No labelled fossil” is the real sentence.

A 20% ancestor of all living people with no unmixed remnant and no named skull is a bigger hole than a 2% ghost. The hole can be ordinary (absorbed, bones in the drawer unmarked). It can be the cave’s reading (left, folded in, or both).

## 4. Basal Eurasian — a different ghost

Still unsampled as an unadmixed person. Not Cousins’ B. Not Takarkori.

**When:** after Out-of-Africa. Split from other Eurasians ~60–50 kya in the models. Visible in Near Eastern farmers ~12–10 kya.

**Who has it:** Near East high; Europe low; East Asia ~0%; most of sub-Saharan Africa ~0%.

**Signature:** little or no Neanderthal DNA.

### Ancient fractions (Lazaridis 2016 unless noted)

|---|---|
| Iran Mesolithic | 66% ± 13% |
| Iran Neolithic | 48% ± 6% (later models often 38–48%) |

Percentages move with the model. The **order** is stable: Gulf/Iran/Levant high, Europe lower, East Asia none.

## 6. The liger lock — scope

Locked. It is about **mating between distant lines**, not about a lion species.

Nephilim / Gibborim = Apkallu men × Lullu women. Two stacked mechanisms (`00_MASTER_TIMELINE` Phase VI; `95` Nephilim):

1. Heterosis — a wide cross makes a bigger, hardier first generation.
2. Divergent imprinting — father’s IGF2 pushes growth; mother’s H19 pushes back. Lines that have been apart have different settings. The child can overshoot. Image: lion father × tiger mother → liger. Tighter paper: Vrana et al. 1998, deer-mouse hybrids.

This lock is **Phase VI**. It does not apply to Cousins’ A×B unless you adopt that as a new decision.

A×B at 300 kya is a sister-hominin cross. If it ran the same mismatch, the first mixed generation might have been large. Cousins does not say that. Living people are not a giant species. B sequence is stripped near genes, not kept as a growth package. AMH at 300 kya is a skull-and-face change, not Nephilim.

## 8. Live options

Pick one from each group. Package suggestions are in §9.

### Decision A — Who is Cousins’ A?

1. **Adamu / the fused harvest.** Fits the bottleneck on A, the fusion on A, and N/D coming from A. Least breakage.
2. **The wild majority, never the harvest.** Then the intervention sits on B. Fights Cousins’ demography (crash is on A).

### Decision B — Who is Cousins’ B?

1. **Unharvested sister hominin.** Dragon hearth-partners; wood, stones, fire. Some hearing grows in the flesh. 20% folded into A at 300 kya. Unfused B (if 48) can leave or die. No new species name required.
2. **A second modification track.** Controllers run B in parallel, more altered, then fold 20% back. B is still a hominin, not the Anunnaki.
3. **Telepaths who leave.** B evolves native field-hearing (dragon road, no installed tap). Some depart. The 20% is stay-behinds, a seed before launch, or a failed launch. A return from space is the loudest version; it starts to look like the old returnee gloss. Prefer stay-behinds or seed.
4. **Engineered super-species (rejected in conversation unless A is swapped).** Fights the bottleneck-on-A and fusion-on-A.

If B is the hearth line, that is a finer name for one branch of the already-locked *erectus*–dragon partnership. It does not add a new labor species.

### Decision C — What is the fusion *for*?

1. **Current lock: upgrade.** Firewall + receiver + god-compatible 46. N/D inherited the upgrade and went feral.
2. **Preserve / freeze.** Fusion holds A so A cannot become B. N/D are the clean archive (fusion, no B). Living humans are frozen A plus a 20% B leak. The seam is a tap on a frozen line, not a gift. You lose “46 so we can breed with the gods” unless you rewrite it: the gods built the clamp to their own count.
3. **Two applications of one scar.** Fusion 1 on A at ~0.9 mya closes the gate. Fusion 2 on B (or on a subset of B) between 0.9 and 0.3 mya opens it. Same hardware, opposite job. The 300 kya join is the readout. Unfused B cannot come home. This is story. Cousins does not find a second fusion.

### Decision D — How do they mix at 300 kya if counts may differ?

1. **B was 46 too.** Easiest genetics. 20% is still large for 1.2 my of isolation (genic mismatches remain).
2. **46×48 leak.** Possible. Harder to get 20%. A’s count wins. B’s sequence hitchhikes.
3. **Second fusion on stay-behind B** (Decision C3). Mixing is turned back on on purpose.

Cousins is silent. Choose on story grounds and keep the honesty line in `90`.

### Decision E — Why did 20% of a hard cross persist?

1. Numbers: B larger, A small after the crash.
2. A short, dense contact.
3. They were karyotypically compatible (D1 or D3).
4. Someone favored the mixed children.

If 4: dragons fit the locked method (which infants live at the hearth). Climate and A’s small size can do the same work. Dragons would be intensifying a filter that already has a terrestrial handle. They do not prove B was 48.

### Decision F — What trips Phase III?

1. **Current:** Neanderthal “wild code” at ~70 kya trips the receiver. Science is weak (Reich; N pulse now ~47 kya; Africans lack most N DNA).
2. **Retarget:** the spark is the 20% B already in everyone since 300 kya. 70–50 kya stays the expansion. Activation = leftover B alleles get used or stop being silenced. N DNA is more freeze, more A. “Wild code” points at B.
3. **Split the jobs:** 300 kya = modern body (A+B). 70–45 kya = expansion + small N/D pulses. Activation remains cave gloss.

F3 is the hygiene move even if B’s story job stays open.
