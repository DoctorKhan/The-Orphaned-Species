## 2. ◐ Protagonist passivity — RESOLVED IN DESIGN: the rise-and-mature (anti-Paul) arc

**Problem.** Eli's dominant mode across all three volumes is **declining**: refuses the ancestry file, chosen-one flattery, chasing captors, deification, the throne, the amplifier, coercion, destroying Lang. Even his central want (find mother) is engineered to convert into *release*. A hero who mostly says no reads as acted-upon, and once the reader clocks the pattern, temptations lose tension (the outcome is pre-known).

**Why it matters.** This is the single biggest *dramatic* risk (distinct from thematic soundness, which is fine). It affects every scene Eli is in.

**Evidence.** `27_character_development_arcs.md` belief→behavior table (every row resolves in a hard no); scene seed "refuses prophecy language once per volume" (`26`); the Vol III double-temptation (`33` Ch 9 — Sol pressing for "just this once more" while Lang's apparatus lobbies in parallel, per the 2026-08-01 live-pressure lock).

**Suggested direction.** Two correctives:
- **Make some hard nos genuinely wrong.** The seed where his restraint gets a woman killed (`49` Scene 0) is exactly this — do more of it. A hard no that costs an innocent is the only thing that makes the *next* limit suspenseful. **Chapter placement not yet re-pinned** after Book III's Act restructure (`33`, `49`).
- **Give him active wins per volume**, not just costly noes — something he *builds/achieves by pursuing*. Vol I has one (protecting the rice community). Surface Vol II's and Vol III's now, or push the Bard/transmission capacity (his one positive power) earlier so three books aren't all renunciation.

**Resolution (LOCKED, 2026-07-12) — recast as ascent, not renunciation.** Eli's through-line is a **rise-and-mature (anti-Paul) arc**: he *achieves* worldly things throughout (competence, allies, a network, wins, a following), so the apex release reads as sacrifice, not temperament. The positive form is named — **structure that breathes**: temporary, self-dissolving authority (the *festival crown*; the *fission–fusion* gather-and-disperse), concentrated only at surplus or necessity, resting on the keystone that **force is a function of disconnection** (connected people self-govern; *only the disconnected need managing*). He **sees the cost of imbalance both ways** (permanent throne = cage; pure renunciation = the innocent his restraint kills, `49` Scene 0). Per-volume ascent in `27`; positive form in `Thesis.md` § "The mature form — structure that breathes"; third-door content in `49` § G; climax enactment in `41` §46. **Status ◐** — design resolved; drafting must prove the ascent is real *and* the descent costs.

**Related:** ties to #1 (the native road is his one active *creation*).
