## Governing rule

The reader must feel the weave on-page, not infer it from outline. Every mythic
object, political maneuver, and personal choice must have a body, a cost, and a
consequence in the same scene it appears.

---

## Fix 1 — Nested Artifact Progression

Artifact decoding answers a tactical question and opens a philosophical nightmare
in the same scene.

**Implementation rule:**
Every decoded artifact must do both of these before the chapter ends:
1. give Eli or the cast a usable tool, route, or warning for the present-day plot;
2. produce metadata, a wrong reading, or a missing link that points to a larger
   lie about the cage.

**False-peak rule — LOCK 2026-08-21:**
In every book Eli must reach at least one moment where he believes he has the
master answer, then learn within the same book that he has only uncovered another
layer of the cage. The false peak must cost someone else.

**Chain of custody across the ten books:**
- Books I–III: artifacts reveal the methods of the Igigi and the biology of the
  receiver. The deepest cosmological layers are withheld from on-page
  certification by characters.
- Books IV–VI: artifacts reveal the mutiny and the control system's origin. The
  reader may reach the full synthesis before Eli does; Eli earns it through
  personal failure.
- Books VII–IX: artifacts reveal the makers and the failing planetary engine.
  The final object is never possessed; it is witnessed or worn out.
- Book X: no final artifact unlocks the ending. The ending is chosen conduct,
  not decoded knowledge.

**Example anchor:**
Books III Forks artifact — tactical win: biological frequency that stabilizes a
local grid failure; philosophical nightmare: isotopic signature does not match
Earth, proving the Chromosome-2 receiver was designed for something older than
the Igigi.

## Fix 2 — The Bleed

No one interacts with the Mesh, a charged ground, or a deep-time artifact without
paying a visible biological tax. The larger the interaction, the worse the
symptom. The medical effects are not magic; they are neurochemical and somatic
consequence of forcing the receiver organ to carry signals it was not built to
hold.

**Level 1 — Listening:** nosebleeds, phantom ozone smell, fine-motor tremors,
temporary tinnitus.
**Level 2 — Pushing:** temporary aphasia or dyslexia, body temperature drop,
vomiting, loss of color vision, blackout spots in vision.
**Level 3 — Forced coherence:** micro-seizures, capillary burst in eyes or nose,
loss of proprioception, loss of bladder control, post-ictal confusion that can
last hours.

**Implementation rule:**
In every book, at least one scene must show a character paying a Level 2 or
Level 3 cost for using the field. The cost must limit the next scene, not reset
by magic between chapters. The Bleed is also the physical proof that Eli's
refusal of power is self-preservation, not moral posturing.

**Grounding rule — LOCK 2026-08-21:**
When a character touches the Mesh or a charged ground, the prose must lead with
smell, heat, exhaustion, or pain. Ozone, burnt sugar, metallic taste, cold
sweat, the weight of stone on the skull, the muscle tremor that drops a cup. The
prose never drifts into glowing, ethereal, or reverent description of "magic."

## Fix 3 — Fractured Ally Arc

Eli, Wren, and Rowan must face a crisis in every two-book cycle where their
primary virtues demand mutually exclusive actions. The reader must be able to
root for all three positions before any one of them loses.

**Cycle map:**
- Books I–II: Wren's procedural safety versus Rowan's immediate care; Eli's
  receiver-read shortcuts Wren's consent protocols and endangers the very people
  Rowan is trying to shelter.
- Books III–IV: Wren's institutional design versus Rowan's embodied trust; Eli's
  wartime command makes him exactly the authority Rowan agreed to share a life
  with, not a person.
- Books V–VI: Wren's keeper-channel discipline versus Rowan's domestic
  refusal to leave anyone behind; Eli's growing breadth makes him unable to stay
  in one place long enough to keep either promise.
- Books VII–IX: Rowan's refusal to let Tara or the son be weaponized versus
  Wren's refusal to hand them over to any state or court; Eli must choose which
  gate to hold.

**Implementation rule:**
Each fracture must last at least one full book after the breaking scene. Trust
cannot be restored by a single conversation; it must be rebuilt through shared
risk, visible cost, and changed behavior across multiple chapters.

## Fix 4 — Charkha Mirror

A named Charkha operative functions as Eli's dark reflection: awakened, capable,
and willing to do the thing Eli refuses. This character must repeatedly save
Eli's life, but always for the wrong reasons.

**Name lock — LOCK 2026-08-21:** Vane.

**Function:**
Vane agrees with Eli that the Mandate is a cage and the Igigi were tyrants.
While Eli wants to dismantle the machine, Vane wants to sit in the captain's
chair. Vane proves that seizing power works, making Eli's refusal harder to
sustain.

**Implementation rule:**
Vane must save Eli's life or the lives of Eli's people at least once per
two-book cycle. Each save must create a new debt that forces Eli to defend or
distance himself from Vane's methods. Vane never monologues; he acts and lets
the cost speak.

**The proof beat:**
When Eli refuses to forcibly synchronize a crowd to save them, Vane does it
instead. Vane saves the crowd, but burns out three carriers in the process.
Vane tells Eli: "Your morality is a luxury purchased with other people's blood.
I saved them; you just kept your hands clean." Eli cannot refute the saved
lives; he can only refuse the method and pay the consequence.

## Cross-book pressure checklist

Before a Book I–X draft is closed, it must show:
1. one artifact beat with false-peak shape;
2. one Bleed scene at Level 2 or higher, with next-scene consequence;
3. one fracture beat among Eli / Wren / Rowan, or among the secondary cast if
   Eli is off-screen;
4. one Vane beat if he is in the book, or one assimilation-axis scene if he is
   not;
5. history that gives Eli a tactical advantage or a warning for the present-day
   plot, not just worldbuilding color.
