> **BOOK I CLIMAX — REVISED AND LOCKED 2026-07-22:** Göbekli is one moral/action climax, not a cosmological or civilizational lecture. > **GÖBEKLI FUNCTION / REVEAL CAP — LOCKED 2026-07-17:** In full canon, Göbekli is a **communication station**, like the other stone circles: it routes live transmission and reception through the planetary conductors. It does **not** store consciousness or amplify the field. Book I does not reveal that answer. By the ending, Eli's group knows only that the buried geometry is a real, responsive interface associated with contact and control; their measurements cannot distinguish receiver, transmitter, archive, or amplifier. The “complete record” at stake means the site's archaeological record plus a clean recording of its activation—not minds or history stored inside the stones. The communication function becomes legible later through the wider circle network.

### Taş Tepeler revelation ladder — LOCKED 2026-07-16

The tepes are not a tour of interchangeable stone circles. They are a distributed argument, with each site carrying one layer of the human/Heaven interface. Only Karahan Tepe and Göbekli receive full dramatic scenes; the other sites arrive through field records, scans, recovered reports, and arguments over interpretation.

- **Çakmaktepe:** ordinary shelter, food, cooperation, and mutual aid before monumentality. Civilization begins as care, not priestly command.
- **Nevalı Çori:** household and communal ritual coexist. The sacred is still embedded in family, work, and the body.
- **Karahan Tepe:** the body as receiver. Architecture cut into the hill, benches, and human imagery give Somchai's breath/posture/resonance practice an ancient precedent. Eli learns that altered perception is disciplined practice, not supernatural status.
- **Sayburç:** human/animal scenes turn symbol into narrative and memory. Myth is an early technology for preserving interspecies relationship.
- **Sefer Tepe, Harbetsuvan, and Taşlı Tepe:** local variation proves the network had no single capital or authorized reading. This undercuts the Custodians' claim that Göbekli alone licenses ownership.
- **Göbekli Tepe:** in authorial canon, a buried communication station whose bodies, animals, symbols, sky, and collective attention once made contact repeatable. To the Book I characters it remains only the most complete surviving interface: the enclosures and associated artifacts preserve a damaged human record of contact and attempted control, but the station's actual function is still unknown.

The on-page sequence therefore moves from **how people survive together** (Çakmaktepe), through **how shared life becomes ritual** (Nevalı Çori), to **how the body learns resonance** (Karahan), and finally to **the unresolved scar of contact becoming selection and command** (Göbekli). Eli encounters the pieces but does not yet possess the family or civilizational interpretation. Göbekli is the visual and moral climax: the buried enclosures, rising pillars, rival teams, and failing route make the choice between the complete record and living people physically overwhelming. Its revelation remains deliberately incomplete; family/loss, the engine/exhaust synthesis, panspermia, dragons, and the full Heaven/Earth design stay for later books.
