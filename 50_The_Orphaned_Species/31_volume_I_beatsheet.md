# Volume I — *The Breach* — Beat Sheet

> **CURRENT SPINE — LOCKED.** Book I runs **the Reclamation, Pelangi Reach → Meridian City → Straits → Melaka → breach → Mekong Delta (agricultural witness) → Gulf/Mesopotamia → Göbekli Tepe (climax)**. Mekong/Vietnam is the substantial agricultural-witness sequence; Thailand a shorter grounding/memory hinge; Bangladesh a brief village/home hinge. **Book I climaxes at Göbekli Tepe:** Eli chooses people over the complete interface record and Emrys is taken. The old Mekong-climax allocation and the old chapter numbering are retired; use the order below.

> **COSMOLOGICAL FUNCTION — LOCKED 2026-07-15:** Book I establishes the divine knowledge-blueprint and the human fear that turns life-technology into a target. The later resolution is not anti-technology: technological understanding must mature enough to recognize the Nephilot/elven line as fellow persons rather than repeat the ancient killing.

> **Pelangi pressure — LOCKED 2026-07-16:** Pelangi Reach is a compromised royal tech-hub project under climate stress, not a preserved island utopia. The king's desalination, AI, flood, cooling, biotech, and robotics systems compete with Meridian City while older water, farming, temple, kinship, and craft institutions keep the island relational. Those institutions are changing, losing land, and being packaged for development; the island is less what it was, but not simply worse.

> **BOOK I CLIMAX — LOCKED 2026-07-16:** Göbekli is one moral/action climax, not a cosmological lecture. The interface race and assault force Eli to choose between preserving the complete record and saving people. He completes **personal Manual Override** himself: he feels the command/charge, refuses autopilot and ownership, and breaks the interface's clean read to protect the living group. The complete record is lost or damaged, **Emrys is taken**, and Eli survives without a total explanation. Book I ends with the human truth—civilization turned knowledge into ownership—and the partial discovery of a powerful male carrier/mighty lineage whose inherited authority became hierarchy. **Nephilim** may appear only as a disputed later archive label, not a species answer. Panspermia, dragons, the full Heaven/Earth design, and the Double-Fork reveal remain for later books.

> **GÖBEKLI FUNCTION / REVEAL CAP — LOCKED 2026-07-17:** In full canon, Göbekli is a **communication station**, like the other stone circles: it routes live transmission and reception through the planetary conductors. It does **not** store consciousness or amplify the field. Book I does not reveal that answer. By the ending, Eli's group knows only that the buried geometry is a real, responsive interface associated with contact and control; their measurements cannot distinguish receiver, transmitter, archive, or amplifier. The “complete record” at stake means the site's archaeological record plus a clean recording of its activation—not minds or history stored inside the stones. The communication function becomes legible later through the wider circle network.

> **ROOTBOOK — LOCKED 2026-07-21:** Maren's encoded comparative field journal enters only after the ring and human witness have established the emotional trail. A damaged scan or folio recovered through the Melaka/Meridian evidence chain reveals a first **name** index and later a **foot/step** correspondence. It points to bounded tests, never the whole pilgrimage. Full design: `52_the_rootbook_word_web.md`.

> **OPENING ORDER — LOCKED:** **Ch 1 Pelangi Reach ordinary life and staged attack → Ch 2 one sustained southern-flight movement: escape through the blocked corridor → brief Meridian City processing and ring photograph → north through the Straits → Melaka witness collision → pursuit and asphyxia breach → Ch 3 aftermath and grounding → Ch 4 Wren's independent extraction and maritime departure**. The breach lands at the **end of Chapter 2**, not after a six-chapter runway. Meridian City remains a sharp pressure chamber inside the flight, not a second opening or a standalone worldbuilding stop. No post-Melaka return to Meridian City. The detailed opening material sits in the **Scene Bank** below; mine it into Ch 2–4 rather than restoring the retired sequence. The Mekong (Ch 5–10) and Gulf/Göbekli (Ch 11–13) chapters then follow.

## ACT IV — Westward Race and Göbekli *(quarry — rebuild chapter count while drafting)*

### Taş Tepeler revelation ladder — LOCKED 2026-07-16

The tepes are not a tour of interchangeable stone circles. They are a distributed argument, with each site carrying one layer of the human/Heaven interface. Only Karahan Tepe and Göbekli receive full dramatic scenes; the other sites arrive through field records, scans, recovered reports, and arguments over interpretation.

- **Çakmaktepe:** ordinary shelter, food, cooperation, and mutual aid before monumentality. Civilization begins as care, not priestly command.
- **Nevalı Çori:** household and communal ritual coexist. The sacred is still embedded in family, work, and the body.
- **Karahan Tepe:** the body as receiver. Architecture cut into the hill, benches, and human imagery give Somchai's breath/posture/resonance practice an ancient precedent. Eli learns that altered perception is disciplined practice, not supernatural status.
- **Sayburç:** human/animal scenes turn symbol into narrative and memory. Myth is an early technology for preserving interspecies relationship.
- **Sefer Tepe, Harbetsuvan, and Taşlı Tepe:** local variation proves the network had no single capital or authorized reading. This undercuts the Custodians' claim that Göbekli alone licenses ownership.
- **Göbekli Tepe:** in authorial canon, a buried communication station whose bodies, animals, symbols, sky, and collective attention once made contact repeatable. To the Book I characters it remains only the most complete surviving interface: the enclosures and associated artifacts preserve a damaged human record of contact and attempted control, but the station's actual function is still unknown.

The on-page sequence therefore moves from **how people survive together** (Çakmaktepe), through **how shared life becomes ritual** (Nevalı Çori), to **how the body learns resonance** (Karahan), and finally to **how intelligence becomes an instrument that can be owned** (Göbekli). Göbekli is the visual and moral climax: the buried enclosures, rising pillars, rival teams, and failing route make the choice between the complete record and living people physically overwhelming. Its revelation remains deliberately incomplete; panspermia, dragons, and the full Heaven/Earth design stay for later books.
