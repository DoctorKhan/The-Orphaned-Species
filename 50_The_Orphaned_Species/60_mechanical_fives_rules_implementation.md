# Mechanical Five-Rules Implementation

> **LOCKED 2026-08-21.** This document translates the five character-humanness
> rules from `15_character_grounding_and_relatability.md` and `16_reader_attachment.md`
> into concrete, mechanical requirements for Volumes I–IV. Each requirement names
> exact manuscript anchors already on disk, so fixes can be applied surgically
> without destabilizing prose.

---

## 1. Mundane Anchor — cross-volume requirement

**Rule:** Every major recurring character carries one tactile, non-plot-essential
hyper-fixation or comfort habit across the series. When tragedy strikes, mourn
the anchor, not the abstraction.

| Character | Locked mundane anchor | Manuscript anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **Eli** | Repairs things carefully; touches metal railings when anxious; keeps a cheap meal ritual; writes practical lists. | `I_The_Breach.md:224` — finishes Mei's cable at dawn; `I_The_Breach.md:122` — tends Rasel's seedlings with thumb-and-foreringer at the base |
| **Wren** | Mends bags/tarps/straps when stressed; checks exits; routes people before admitting she needs one. | `I_The_Breach.md:1728` — "Wren mends a tear in a tarp with the same face she uses for manifests"; `II_The_Descent.md:10943` — resumes the stitch |
| **Rowan** | Corridor ink stain on cuff; leaf paste while concentrating; pencil-shavings smell in her room. | `IV_The_Court_of_Threads.md:138` — pencil shavings, hot water, leaf paste; `IV_The_Court_of_Threads.md:155` — ink stain on cuff |
| **Somchai** | Washes hands too long; checks patients after hours; falls asleep at meals; says no before explaining why. | `II_The_Descent.md:4390` — nurse/patient body language; `II_The_Descent.md:3896` — soap/palms/backs/between-the-fingers ritual |
| **Mei** | Free-meal list without asking who's on it; terrace table; coconut and cleaver. | `I_The_Breach.md:142` — terrace table set for one; `I_The_Breach.md:154` — green coconut, cleaver |
| **Suresh** | Net/harbor work; flat Malay commands; coiling rope; frayed-sheet inspection. | `I_The_Breach.md:48` — Suresh's hand over Eli's on the tiller; `I_The_Breach.md:94` — frayed sheet, clean cut, thumbnail inspection |
| **Bima** | Repaired harness with green sail thread; refuses to discard anything that can still embarrass its manufacturer. | `III_The_Compact.md:18` — safety harness repaired with green sail thread |

**Mechanical requirement for future volumes:**
- When a death/disaster occurs, at least one beat must show the survivor attending to the anchor object/habit before any larger reflection. No monologue about loss without the mundane physical act in the same scene or adjacent scene.

## 2. Weaponized Competence — competence-as-violation beat

**Rule:** The exact trait readers love a character for must be the thing that wounds
someone they love. Add one such beat per volume.

| Volume | Character | Strength | Violation | Manuscript anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Wren** | Cold, precise routing/escort | Routes Eli through Singapore without telling him the full cost of the manifest change. | `I_The_Breach.md:355` — Wren adds contractor bundle without being asked |
| **II** | **Eli** | Receiver read / pattern-sight | Uses partial contact to push Sora's collar into containment rather than remove it. | `II_The_Descent.md:6322` / `II_The_Descent.md:7215` — central operator smoothing |
| **III** | **Eli** | Bounded wartime command / smoothing | Smooths Rowan's anger through a contact read because it reads like a broken circuit to him. | `III_The_Compact.md:5702` — "Enough to know he could smooth it away." |
| **III** | **Wren** | Intake logistics | Optimizes a schedule that prevents Rowan from saying goodbye to a dying contact. | `III_The_Compact.md:3772–3812` — Rowan calls Eli without Wren; Wren's route notice held in outbox while Rowan has already acted |

**Mechanical requirement:**
- In each volume's beatsheet, add a line: `Competence-as-violation beat: [character] uses [strength] against [person] in [chapter/context].`
- The scene must end with the violator either realizing too late or choosing not to undo the harm. No instant forgiveness.

## 5. Shifting V Dynamic — rotating isolation

**Rule:** Rotate the isolation. In every book, a different pairing aligns while
the third is left out for operational reasons.

| Phase | Paired | Isolated | Manifest anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A (I–II)** | Eli + Wren — shoulder-to-shoulder routing/tactical | Rowan not yet present / Wren displaced | `II_The_Descent.md:3772` — Rowan calls Eli without Wren |
| **B (III)** | Rowan + Wren — network/house/administrative bond | Eli excluded from domestic peace | `III_The_Compact.md:3772–3812` — Rowan/Eli call; Wren holds route notice in outbox |
| **C (IV)** | Eli + Rowan — pregnancy/labor/domestic decision | Wren shut out of the birth room | `IV_The_Court_of_Threads.md:93` — montage omits Wren; `IV_The_Court_of_Threads.md:7177` — Wren not there |

**Mechanical requirement:**
- In each volume's relationship section, add `Rotated dyad:` and `Isolated third:` with the operational reason, not malice.
- The isolated character must have a visible, mundane cost (not just pouting). In `III` it is Wren holding a route notice in her outbox; in `IV` it is Wren at the clinic/road while Rowan's corridor happens without her.

## Cross-volume mechanical tests

1. **Mundane-anchor check:** For every death/disaster scene in Volumes I–IV, list the anchor object/habit attended to before any abstraction about loss.
2. **Competence-as-violation check:** Every volume must have at least one named scene where the protagonist's superpower harms a loved one.
3. **Scar-visibility check:** Every named scar must affect at least one physical action per volume.
4. **Outside-in check:** Every volume must have one scene tagged `Outside-in POV:` in the beatsheet.
5. **Dyad-rotation check:** Every volume must have `Rotated dyad:` and `Isolated third:` locked in the relationship section.
