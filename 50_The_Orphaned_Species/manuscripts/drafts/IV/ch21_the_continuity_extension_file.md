## Chapter 21—The Continuity Extension File
The extension file arrives at 07:22. It is a priority packet. The priority label says EMERGENCY CONTINUITY — DO NOT DELAY. The packet says Eli's mandate has been automatically extended for thirty days. The packet says this extension complies with the provisional court's continuity clause. The packet says the clause is optional and reversible. The clause is now irreversible.

Asha receives the packet. She is a Continuity officer. She is not Eli's officer. She is the officer who audits the compact's emergency powers. She reads the packet. She reads it twice. She reads it a third time.

The first reading tells her the mandate has been extended. The second reading tells her the clause has been invoked. The third reading tells her the extension is binding. It says that Eli's authority is now permanent unless the court overrules it within fourteen days. The court does not overrule. The court does not exist yet.

Asha sits in her office. The office is small. It has a desk and a window that looks out on the harbor. The harbor is empty. It is 07:22. The boats are moving. The water is gray.

She opens the packet. She reads the clause. She reads the protocol. She reads the signature. The signature is forged. The forger used a copy of Eli's signature that was filed in the compact's public archive. The archive is public. The forger had access to the archive. The forger had access to the court's Continuity officer.

Asha closes the packet. She picks up the phone. She calls the Continuity officer. The officer is not in the office. The officer is on the route. The officer does not answer.

Asha waits. She does not wait long. She picks up the packet and walks to the registrar's office. The registrar is a woman named Mina. Mina is the same Mina who was the attending physician at the hospital in Book III. She is on the court's medical board now. She is the one who files the emergency powers.

Asha puts the packet on the desk. Mina does not look at the packet. She looks at Asha. She says, "What is it?"

"It's an extension," Asha says. "It's bound. It's citing the clause. It's been filed as irreversible."

Mina reads the signature. She says, "Who signed it?"

"No one," Asha says. "It was forged. It was filed by someone who had access to the public archive."

Mina writes the name on the page. She writes it slowly. The pen scratches. She writes the date. She writes the protocol number. She writes the name of the Continuity officer who filed it.

Then she says, "The officer is on a route. The officer is not available. The extension is now on file. It is effective until the court meets."

Asha stands. She says, "The court does not meet until Tuesday. That is fourteen days."

Mina does not look at her. She says, "Then Ell will have a mandate for fourteen days."

Asha leaves. She goes to the court anteroom. She finds Eli. She does not tell him about the forgery. She tells him about the extension. She tells him it is on file. She tells him it is effective until the court meets. She tells him it is binding.

Eli reads the packet. He does not argue. He does not deny it. He says, "Who filed it?"

Asha says, "A Continuity officer. The officer said it was emergency. The officer said the clause was invoked."

Eli folds the packet. He says, "I did not invoke it."

Asha says, "I know."

She leaves. She goes to the registrar's office. She finds Mina. She asks for the protocol number. Mina gives it to her. She asks for the name of the Continuity officer. Mina gives it to her. She asks for the signature. Mina gives it to her. She holds the paper. She looks at the signature. She does not say anything. She just holds the paper.

Matthew 23:23: you give a tenth of your spices of mint, of dill, and of cumin. But you neglect the weightier matters of justice, mercy, and faithfulness. The extension file gives continuity. It neglects review.

Tao 78: true words seem paradoxical. The forger's signature is a copy of Eli's. The copy is not the original. The copy makes the clause irreversible. The original made it optional.

Asha returns to the office. She opens the packet. She does what the protocol requires. She suspends the officer. She flags the file. She writes the suspension in the permanent record. She writes the date. She writes the name. She writes the reason: forgery.

The officer is suspended. Eli's calendar is protected for one more week. The faction shifts to a faster-track vote.

The extension does not remove Eli's authority. The insertion of the clause does.
