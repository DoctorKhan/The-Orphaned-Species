# Psionic Combat — The Tactical System

*The rules of psionic conflict, so set pieces can be drafted without inventing mechanics mid-scene. Answers Priority-1 #5 in `99_open_questions.md`. Built on the energetic limits in `05_story_architecture.md` §3 and the charge taxonomy / war texture in `/00_NARRATIVE_STRUCTURE.md`. Terminology: **psionic / psions**, never "psychic."*

> **RENDERING RULE — read first.** The primitives are mechanically real and they **are visible — but only on the psi plane, to those with the sight.** They are not a public light show. There are two perceptual layers, and the prose renders whichever one the POV occupies:
>
> - **Ordinary perception** (anyone without the sight; anyone watching from outside): sees no forms — only the **material consequences.** A man staggers, goes blank, bleeds from the nose; the air feels close; a room turns calm or wrong. To a phone camera there is nothing to film. This is what keeps the surface un-spectacular and honors the canon's "no superhero duel" promise *for the world at large*.
> - **Psi-plane perception** (Eli, Wren, any psion or seer holding POV): actually **sees the forms** — the skin of a bubble, a lance driven home as a line, a block sitting in the front of a skull, a severed channel whipping back on its owner. When the POV character can see the plane, render the geometry; it is genuinely what they perceive.
>
> The error to avoid is not *visible forms* — it is forms visible to *everyone*, a light show on the material plane. The war is fought on a plane most people cannot see ("men moved by forces they can't perceive"). **POV decides the register.**

> **GROUNDING RULE.** Psionic action never replaces the material world. Radios, boats, food, sleep, weapons, medicine, border records, weather, and trusted human scouts still decide outcomes. A psionic victory can open a gate; someone still has to walk through it. Every operation is joined to ordinary intelligence work, transport, and human betrayal.

## 1. The resource — coherence, not a battery

The anti-entropic field is **not a finite power supply** (`05` §3). What a psion spends is **coherence**: the ability to hold attention as one steady, undivided pattern under pressure. Everything below costs coherence, and coherence is bounded by attention, biological stability, sleep, pain, fear, and emotional exposure.

**Strength** in any contest = **coherence (skill/practice)** × **energy supplied** (how hard you push) × **coherent minds acting as one** (a team) × **proximity to a conductor/node** (a ley line, a stone, a relay amplifies everything). This single formula decides who shields, who breaks, who reflects.

This is why psions fight in **teams**: coherent attention pooled is greater than the sum, and a team can hold, probe, shield, or overwhelm what no lone psion can. It is also why the **fellowship** matters thematically — collective coherence without a command hierarchy is the coalition's whole problem (`05` §7).

## 2. The four primitives

### A. The Bubble — coherence field (defense)

A held region in which hostile attention can't get purchase. Inside: clarity, quiet, the body's pattern steady. Outside: the pressure of whatever is pushing on it.

- **Solo:** can shield self, barely; thins fast under sustained pressure.
- **Expand by supplying more energy** (user's mechanic): a bubble can be pushed outward to cover companions or a room — but **volume thins density**. A wide bubble is a weak bubble unless more coherent minds feed it. This is the literal mechanic of "stand close, hold together."
- **Team:** several psions in concert hold a bubble both *wider and denser* — the coalition's signature defense.
- **Cost:** continuous coherence drain; the holder can't also attack hard while holding a large shield. Drop your attention and the bubble collapses.
- **Counter:** the Break (§D), or simply outlasting the holder's stamina.

### B. The Lance — directed intrusion (attack)

A focused line of hostile attention driven into a target: the **weaponized push** (negative charge / aversion). Felt as cold, a hook set in the chest, the body's self-maintenance disrupted — at strength it produces real bodily consequence (pain, disorientation, collapse, injury), because the field helps maintain biology and a hostile line disrupts that pattern.

- **Requires a signature lock** (§3) and, for range, a **conductor** (§3). Up close, eye contact or touch is conductor enough.
- **Reflection — bend it back on the originator** (user's mechanic): the Lance is a **two-way channel**. The attacker has opened a line to you; a skilled defender who recognizes the incoming line can **invert the channel** and send the disruption back down it into the attacker. High skill — you must catch it rather than merely brace against it. If the attacker feels the inversion coming, they can **sever their own line** to escape it (cost: their attack ends, signature briefly exposed). Reflection is the masterstroke, and it is thematically the **undeceivability** move: turning the manipulation back on the manipulator.
- **The soft suite (already canon).** Beside the hard Lance sit the intimate attacks: flooding with fear/memory/noise, **false certainty**, dream-intrusion, forced identification with information that isn't yours, disrupting a group's shared attention, marking a target for a physical recovery team. These are the *quiet* weapons — often more dangerous than the Lance because the victim doesn't know they're under attack.

### C. The Block — prefrontal suppression (disable)

A shield placed **in the front of the target's brain** (user's mechanic) — the prefrontal seat of conscious override — that jams their capacity. Felt as the lights going out in your own head: you can't reach the thing you do, your attention won't cohere, you fall back onto reflex.

- **Thematic lock — this IS the book's spine.** A Block forces the target onto **autopilot**: the conscious player can't step in, so conditioning runs the body (the Manual Override / NPC-vs-player metaphor, weaponized). Crane's whole suppression apparatus is the Block at civilizational scale — *pathologize, discredit, disappear*; the combat Block is its acute, one-on-one form.
- **Counter — the native road is the defense.** You break a Block by re-finding the **inner ring / the watcher behind thought** (Aru's stillness; the Three-Circles center). The combat counter and the spiritual practice are the *same move*: the thing that makes you undeceivable is the thing that makes you un-jammable. This is how the system rewards the practice without making it a power-up.
- **Cost:** holding a Block on a resisting mind is expensive and occupies the attacker; a team can pin a strong target while others act.

### D. The Break — overpowering a shield (escalation)

A Bubble or Block **can be broken if you are strong enough** (user's mechanic) — strength per the §1 formula. A lone strong psion can shatter a weak bubble; a coordinated team can break a strong one.

- **Cost / risk:** breaking is loud. It **exposes the breaker's signature** to everyone nearby, invites reflection, and risks **feedback** if the shield inverts at the moment of collapse. Nothing is permanently safe — every shield buys *time*, not immunity. No team compels anyone absolutely.

## 3. Meta-rules (range, lock, team)

- **Signature lock.** You can't reliably attack what you can't find. Reliable distance work needs a **known signature** — a target you've touched, tracked, or been given. Strangers are hard; the famous, the recently-breached (Eli after Melaka), and the careless are easy. Masking, decoys, and false signatures are core tradecraft.
- **Conductor / range.** Without a conductor, psionic action is **short-range** (a room, a line of sight, a touch). A **ley line, stone, node, or living relay** extends it — sometimes vast distances. This is why the grid matters and why monitored boundaries (the England ambush) work: cross an instrumented threshold and you light up a conductor that a prepared team is already locked onto.
- **Team functions.** A designed team divides labor: one **locks** (holds the signature), one or more **lance**, one **shields** the team, one **scouts/masks**. This is the template for the recurring rival team (`05` §5).
- **Detection ≠ identity.** Sensing a signature reveals direction, intensity, and anomaly — usually **not** a full identity or motive. You know something woke up; you don't yet know who or why.
- **The sight is its own faculty.** Perceiving the psi plane — seeing the forms, not just feeling them — is a *developed* capacity (Eli's "seeing" stage; the pineal interface), not standard issue. A psion may only **feel** a Lance land while a **seer sees** its line, its origin, and the lock behind it. Seers therefore hold a tactical edge: they spot a signature lock forming, read a team's geometry, and catch an incoming line in time to reflect it (§B). Always render combat through what *this* POV can actually perceive — feel, sight, or both.

## 4. Costs, physiology, recovery

- Coherence drains with use; spent psions are **slow, sick, sleepless, and stupid** until they recover. Recovery needs rest, food, safety, and time — ordinary care, which the Healer can support but not shortcut.
- Severe Lance damage and broken Blocks leave **real bodily aftermath**: nosebleeds, fever, tremor, memory gaps, the disruption of a chronic pattern (Eli's TBI flares under attack).
- **Loss of differentiation** is the signature cost for a receiver: under heavy pressure Eli stops being able to tell his own information from foreign information — the Seven leak in. For him, every fight is also an identity fight.

## 5. Failure modes (build set pieces around these)

- **Decoy / misidentification** — you lance the wrong signature; a planted false certainty walks you into a trap.
- **Contamination** — teammates' attention bleeds together; one member's fear or memory infects the unit's coherence.
- **Fracture** — a team holding one big effort shatters when members disagree, panic, or carry conflicting emotion.
- **Exposure** — every hard action (Break, Lance, Block) broadcasts your signature; the hunter becomes findable.
- **Feedback** — reflection and collapsing shields send disruption back into the source.

## 6. Worked micro-examples (canon set pieces)

- **Melaka breach.** No combat yet — Eli's near-death breach *lights a conductor* and broadcasts an uncontrolled signature for miles. Two teams that were already contesting a carrier/route now have a lock on him. The breach is the dinner bell (already drafted in the Wren escape).
- **Maritime boarding (Strait).** Deniable psions travel *with* a human boarding party. One masks the boat's approach (soft suite); one lances the helmsman into autopilot error (Block) while pirates do the physical work. Wren must tell a human tactic from an intrusion in her own perception — a §5 false-certainty problem. Boats, weather, and weapons still decide it.
- **England boundary ambush.** Crossing an instrumented threshold lights a conductor; a prepared team already in position locks the signature and opens with Lances before anyone can explain the site. The defense is a team Bubble held while they get off the conductor — procedural, not mystical (`05` §6, "monitored boundaries").
- **Albion climax.** Holding collective coherence (a team Bubble around the node) without it becoming a command hierarchy — the coalition's coherence vs. several teams trying to Break in, Block the holders, or turn the shared field to domination.

## 7. Thematic locks (why these mechanics, not generic ones)

1. **Block = forced autopilot.** Combat enacts the thesis: the enemy's core move is to switch off your conscious override; freedom is re-finding the player behind the NPC.
2. **Reflection = undeceivability.** The defining counter is turning manipulation back on the manipulator — the intimate version of the whole book's argument.
3. **Bubble = coalition.** Defense scales only with coherent minds acting as one without a commander — exactly the political form Volume III must prove.
4. **The native road is the armor.** The practice that makes you free is the same practice that keeps you un-jammed. Power is never separable from the inner work, so the system can't become a superhero kit.
