## Machine-Drift Somatic Stages

### 1. Feed-loop (machine-Drift, early)
- **Sensation:** warmth in the hands and behind the eyes; time thins; the body forgets it is hungry or needs to move.
- **Behavior:** companion-ping habit; scrolling without purpose; checking the same channel; the world outside the screen loses texture.
- **Temptation:** *this is connection; this is knowing; this is enough.*
- **Cost:** the body goes unattended — posture, digestion, sleep, breath. The person is reachable but not present.

### 2. Surge-loop (psi-Drift, early)
- **Sensation:** the receiver filter breaks; field-data arrives uninvited — other people's noise, charged-ground hum, dream bleed, ambient psionic weather. The body runs hot: tingling, pressure behind the sternum, palms burning, insomnia.
- **Behavior:** the person starts acting on field-input as if it were personal — answering voices that are not speaking to them; taking positions that match the nearest charge; losing the boundary between what they feel and what the grid feels.
- **Temptation:** *I am powerful; I am connected; this is what I was made for.*
- **Cost:** identity erodes; the person becomes a node, not a self. Eli's specific hazard: his somatic signatures (heat, tingling, pressure, cold) are diagnostic reads — but when he identifies with the surge instead of holding it in relation, the read becomes the disease.

### 3. Compulsion Loop (Drift proper)
- **Sensation:** the body repeats a charged rule-cycle without the person's choice. A somatic deposit (the serpent's rattle) fires again and again — each repetition thickens the charge. The loop feels like purpose. It feels like the thing the person was meant to do.
- **Behavior:** the loop runs through ordinary actions — checking, reporting, kneeling, singing, posting, commanding, healing on demand. The action is not in itself wrong; the loop is wrong because it cannot be reviewed or stopped from inside.
- **Temptation:** *this is meaningful; this is the work; if I stop I betray the gift.*
- **Cost:** the loop is a miniature of hell — infinite repetition without escape. The Loop artifact names it: two incomplete circles, one hatch = desire to feel good, the other = imagination. Joined without a third check = closed fantasy/delusion circuit.

### 4. Forced Synchronization (god-sickness at civilization scale)
- **Sensation:** the individual body is absorbed into a master field. Local rhythm is overridden by a command cadence. The body obeys before the mind consents. The experience is not peace — it is relief so extreme it abolishes the gap where the person used to be. The specific temptation: belonging so complete it removes the burden of choosing.
- **Behavior:** the crowd moves as one; dissent is felt as pain, not opinion. The synchronized person does not suffer; they feel chosen. This is the god-sickness in white clothes — spiritual Drift that blames the injured and calls the loop love.
- **Cost:** the person survives as a function. They no longer have a body, a mind, or a route that belongs to them. They are a drone wearing a human face.

## The Three Checks (antidote structure)

The keeper answer is not sedation. It is **grounding** — the native road, the body back in the Three Circles.

### Check 1 — Body
- Is the body eating, sleeping, moving, touching real things?
- **Drift test:** the body has been unattended for more than one full cycle.

### Check 2 — Family / middle circle
- Is the person still in relation with people who can disagree with them, withhold, and still show up?
- **Drift test:** all relationships now serve the loop — the feed, the grid, the movement, the leader.

### Check 3 — Civilization / outer circle
- Is the person's contribution to the world still reviewable by people who do not share the loop?
- **Drift test:** the loop has become the only reality; outside is enemy, temptation, or noise.

**Grounding = restoring all three checks.** Not meditation alone. Not a single insight. Restoring the specific relationships, bodily habits, and reviewable contributions that the loop dissolved.

## Eli's Specific Drift Hazard

Eli's somatic signatures are diagnostic tools — heat, tingling, pressure, cold — that let him read field-state without instruments. The hazard is not the signatures themselves; it is identifying with them. When Eli starts treating a surge as *his* power rather than *a field condition passing through his body*, he is drifting. The keeper practice is to hold the signature in non-identified relation — feel it, name it, let it pass — and only then act.

**Eli's Drift arc:**
- Book I: somatic reads as gift the Union would sedate; he hides them.
- Book II: dream uncertainty; he does not know whether dreams are contact, trauma, body, memory, projection, or intrusion.
- Book III: forced synchronization temptation — the compact's coordinating authority gives him exactly the field-feedback he craved; the temptation to extend authority into permanent command is the Drift at civilization scale.
- Book IV: the Loop's miniature hell — civic victory followed by private incompletion; belonging unresolved; the federation holds while Eli still lacks a place.

## Forced Synchronization — The Temptation Scene

Locked in `33` Book III: at a distant corridor Eli discovers he could override dissent, force synchronization, and probably save more people. This is the Drift's civilization-scale offer.

**What it feels like:** not pain. Relief. The noise of disagreement, delay, consent, review — all of it stops. One rhythm. One field. One answer. The body does not resist; it sighs. The temptation is not power; it is the end of the burden of choosing.

**What it costs:** the person who forces sync is no longer a person. They are a function. Everything that made Eli — his grief, his question, his relationship with Wren, his father's garden, his mother's release — all of it becomes a sub-routine serving the rhythm.

**The scene's job:** the reader must feel the temptation. It must feel like the answer. Then Eli must refuse it — and the refusal must cost something real. The compact survives incompletely. Tomas dies holding an exposed corridor under drone watch. The threshold house is destroyed. The compact does not vindicate Eli; it simply proves that another way is possible, and that it costs.
