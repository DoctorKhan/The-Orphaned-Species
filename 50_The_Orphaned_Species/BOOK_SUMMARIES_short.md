## BOOK I — *THE BREACH*

Eli Holt loses his made home at Pelangi Reach after Mei reveals that the family holiday of his childhood was a flight. His parents left him with her for one night, carried their bags toward a rendezvous, and never arrived. No one witnessed their death; Babaji closed the file to keep the search from reaching Eli. Friday's harbor skiff race wins him a weekend cash job: **Cube show facilities** for an idol concert before Singapore Circuit night (Foundry contractor sleeve = corridor cover only). At the same dinner Mei adds one warm, undertechnical detail—his mother once had Singapore work and brought him along as a baby—grief-color only; it detonates in Book III. Flight through Singapore puts an old Mandate intake frame in front of him: Rasel's scarred hand beside the two-metal wedding ring—*same soil*—proving he survived the disappearance. Sunday afternoon concert work and Wren's second-set wardrobe hold open the escape corridor; Circuit-night freight gets him out. Malacca opens a dangerous receiver in Eli and costs Hassan (and the physical ring) to Custodian capture.

At a kelong aftermath, a damaged archive wafer reveals seven images from **Maren's field journal**, encoded partly through word-correspondences; the disputed **Three Circles** tablet remains Babaji's plain personal gift. Eli chooses not to go dark. In Thailand, a river cooperative teaches him that living people, crops, and disagreement can measure what no faction may own. The cooperative holds the physical *Shabdajal*, locally corrected, and grants bounded transport custody after the evacuation. Kitt's fork map wakes a four-day-old maintenance handshake bearing Rasel's initials and watering count—the first current trace that he lives.

Anika Voss offers biometric access to Rasel in exchange for Eli's clean scan. Shireen sends the living garden; Eli gives her a childhood memory of his parents keeping him safe. He refuses Bangladesh refuge and the protected line, accepting a human corridor whose cost lands on Dara's permit. At Sanxingdui, Chinese conservation evidence and Rasel's Mandate coupling apparatus break Maren's field notebook's **language-tree model** into a distributed living mesh. Eli rejects one master rhythm, completes personal Manual Override through consenting mismatched cadences, and confronts Rasel. Wren authenticates the civilian evacuation and pays publicly; Qiao loses clearance. In the greenhouse, Rasel gives Eli Maren's two-winters-old correction: *ask me why I stayed.* The reunion remains loving, furious, and incomplete.

**Book I exit:** Rasel present; Wren displaced but physically present; Qiao outside the institution she served; Maren alive but unrecovered; Maren's field notebook open but unfinished; distributed Tree recognized. Göbekli / Mesopotamia belong to Book II.

## BOOK III — *THE LIVING WORLD* / Living Root

After Maren's release, the awakening spreads—ley lines reactivating, latents developing by proximity, threshold care scaling into contested infrastructure—exactly when the registry argument needs its best evidence. After Albion, the cast crosses the Atlantic into **Belém do Pará** (Guajará Bay / Ver-o-Peso receiving network); Pelangi remains a remote home-node. A pursued refugee boat arrives; Eli's successful temporary coordination makes everyone want to make him permanent. He sponsors one disputed refugee under a voluntary plan (Umul / purpose-≠-usefulness warning surfaces as scrap first); that person commits a serious nonconsensual mind-entry and an ordinary person dies. The harm is real and not Lang's fault. Pelangi remains an active home-node through Mei, cargo, songs, and testimony rather than Eli's physical location.

Lang's Standard becomes attractive through Brazilian clinics, ports, insurers, and security systems after Sol's pressure and the fatal failure of welcome. A contested Eagle–Condor teaching offers hemispheric cooperation without merger; Brazilian participants reject its flattening binaries. Union **Devolution** / survival-roster continuity reaches the room through delegates and records and rhymes with “gods who never went home.” The Union is already a **hybrid** (civic costume + Continuity capacity), so Mandate strikes expose breaks rather than invent them. The Union fractures at a distance and sends incompatible demands south. Mandate and Union strikes ignite the **Awakening War**—a drone war that still forces humans into exposed holding, repair, extraction, and rearguard roles. Men die disproportionately not because only they fight, but because culture still expects them to stay exposed longest. Scarcity of surviving men begins here.

Wren refuses Eli jurisdiction over her threshold network; Brazilian, Pelangi, clinic, threshold-house, Union-remnant, and other communities co-author a bounded emergency compact and accept Hawaiʻi's invitation to use, not own, a damaged Pacific facility. At Hawaiʻi, Eli discovers he could force synchronization, probably save more people, and refuses. The basalt station transmits a growers' seed ledger and then the disputed Forks artifact. Extras divide among Sol, consent-bound defense, mutual aid, and refusal; a perimeter drone strikes a man who came to ask rather than raid. A holding action elsewhere falls disproportionately on men; a named life is lost; a threshold house is destroyed. Extra men, veterans, Harbor Thread elders, and Eli co-found men's cloisters whose standing does not depend on household or romantic acceptance. Rowan and Eli's public partnership carries a faint charge that remains fully readable as political trust.

Sol's continuous field fractures when exhausted members leave; his body fails
and compact medics admit him under Diego Méndez, with an ordinary conduct guard
at the door. Sora dies holding the corridor. Tommy transfers the complete
Custodian mission log, including his own offense. Anika reaches for the record
and is arrested after her remaining officers step out of her field.

**The Loop** (Incomplete Twin Circles) lands its working reading: two incomplete circles—desire to feel good and imagination—that join into an infinity without a third check become a closed fantasy circuit; also the felt consequence of atomization. Book III's constitutional victory is **real and complete in the room where it happens**, but **incomplete** at wider scale: Continuity keys, factional mandate, and private force outside that room stay live and unresolved, so the interregional compact the war seemed to prove was never actually intact. The Loop names both Eli's private incompletion—usefulness without belonging, recognition without home—and the compact's unresolved material debt. It does not invalidate the civic goods that were genuinely built.

Eli begins the inquiry and completes a real constitutional transfer in that hall — distributed and redundant enough that no single attack could reach it. But that win is local: Continuity keys, factional mandate, and private force in regions outside that room stay live and unresolved, so the wider interregional compact the war seemed to prove was never actually intact. The handover Eli made is whole; the wider claim it seemed to imply is not. Book IV opens on discovering how much of that wider compact was never actually there.

**Book III exit:** the ruler prepared to release power has released it — a genuine, completed act in that hall. What the room's win does not secure is the wider interregional compact it seemed to prove; the work of discovering and building that out passes to Book IV. Rowan/Eli remains an unrealized possibility. Book IV inherits a wider compact that looked whole and must be built out under scarcity.

## BOOK IV — *THE COURT OF THREADS* / Thread Loom *(working title)*

Book IV moves principally through Rowan and Wren. The emergency court turns access, placement, medical privacy, household recognition, rumor, ceremony, and delayed messages into instruments of rule. The Weavers control much of trust and passage; the men's cloisters hold defense, reconstruction, veteran loyalty, and civic standing. Both are internally divided. Rowan and Eli consciously recognize attraction and have sex several times before political work, post-sex care, and missing one another make the bond romantic. They desire each other directly; Wren's availability is not the cause.

The court remains in North America during the Lean Year. Some northern families, patients, apprentices, and work crews move through a locally governed Costa Rican receiving-and-transfer Turning toward Brazilian, Caribbean, and Pacific nodes established by Book III. Costa Rica is Central America, not a blank southern refuge. Its corridor was grown over decades from watersheds, farms, nurseries, clinics, ports, and connected tree cover; shade, water, slope stability, food trees, and restoration labor set real admission limits. Its communities demand records on their own terms and sometimes refuse the court. Sol and Continuity officials misuse Eagle–Condor language to demand hemispheric fusion or southern resources; Brazilian and Costa Rican participants insist that flying together preserves distinct authority and the right to refuse. The central cast does not escape the court by fleeing south.

Women already occupy much of the work once coded male—freight, construction, maintenance, perimeter defense, drones, transport, and command—because pre-I wars damaged both sexes **and** civic sorting discarded many male survivors (**Extra surplus**), not because a Paraguay-scale scarce-male world already elevated remaining men. Book III's war begins true scarcity; Lean Year convoy defense, infrastructure repair, and long-route service expose and deepen it without an overnight cull. Cloisters do not become obsolete: when men were Extra they kept them from being nothing; when men become scarce they keep them from being property. Court conflict turns on aptitude, consent, rotation, recovery, care, and who receives public standing—not sex-caste restoration or consort farms.

Rowan has wanted motherhood independently of Eli. During their early sexual relationship she does not want pregnancy and uses contraception; sex is not an audition for fatherhood. Only after romance does she choose Eli as co-parent and decide when to pursue conception. Pregnancy then becomes a succession weapon. Factions try to name her consort, the child heir, and Wren mistress or official; protection approaches custody. Wren fears the Rowan/Eli biological future may make her peripheral. The three romances remain distinct, and Wren's parental role is negotiated separately. The Loop may re-read in court as succession/belonging-as-completion fantasy without becoming a new quest object.

During Rowan's labor, the reconstituted assembly reaches succession while a court faction moves to recognize the child as heir — **same hours**, not sequential admin. Wren faces outward for Rowan; Eli remains lover and father rather than reclaiming the center. The Weavers certify a reviewable process instead of bloodline and accept limits on attestation; constitutional cloisters accept civilian distributed command. Eli activates the prepared transfer before the child's first breath.

**Series exit:** the child has no title or inherited claim. The Weavers and cloisters retain civic standing without becoming hidden sovereign or personal army. Eli is wanted but not indispensable.
