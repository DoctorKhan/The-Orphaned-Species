## Two tiers — dialogue vs scripture

Memorable culture spreads two ways in this project. **Do not collapse them.**

| Tier | Register | Model | Lives in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Dialogue refrains** (this doc) | Character law under cost; named speaker; concrete job | Godfather, Dune, Shakespeare | Novel scenes |
| **B — Lay verses** | Tao-like brevity; image + paradox; no prophet | Tao Te Ching, Dhammapada, Thomas, Proverbs | Companion, zine, epigraphs → **`54_the_lay_verses.md`** |

**Tier A** is spoken by Mei, Suresh, Wren, Emrys. **Tier B** is *found* — kitchen wall, teahouse tile, field notebook margin — unbranded, teaching-without-teaching. The novel dramatizes Tier B as **practice** (cup, watch, meal); it does not recite Tier B as sermon (`25`, `14`).

When the user asks for "quotable like Tao Te Ching," they mean **Tier B**. When sharpening a scene line, mean **Tier A**.

## Governing rule (Tier A — in-scene)

Quotables succeed when they are **compressed law under cost**, not thesis in dialogue.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| One breath; concrete noun; named cost | Faux-scripture in novel prose; omniscient wisdom |
| Character-specific **job** (Mei = hearth, Suresh = freight) | Interchangeable keeper speeches |
| Plant early; repeat with changed meaning | Explain the line in the next sentence |
| Invent vocabulary for real inner experiences | Lore dumps dressed as aphorism |
| Let Tier B verses stay in companion layer | Have characters recite lay scripture as exposition |

The novel **invites experience; it does not recruit** (`14` § Reader promise). Spreadable **dialogue** should work out of context without asking the reader to kneel to Eli. Spreadable **verses** (`54`) should work without any character attached.

**Messiah on-page vs anti-cult in companion (LOCKED 2026-07-23):** the **story runs the messiah shape to completion** — by Book III reasonable people treat Eli as Deiwos/chosen one (`29`, `33`). Anti–Law 27 guards here govern **what we meme to readers** (lay teachings, federation constitution), **not** whether the novel shows pilgrims, chalk, throne offers, and *Son of Man* rumor. **Do meme** the counter-instruments. **Do not meme** capture-side doctrine as fan merch — but **do write** Sol, crowds, and registry on-page.

**Anti–Law 27 guard:** do not meme the capture side (Deiwos circles as fan merch, Sol's blood logic, registry cool). Meme the **lay teachings** (Tier B first, Tier A second) and **federation constitution**.

## The three-beat shape

Most sticky lines use **setup → turn → land** (Godfather) or **not X but Y** (Shakespeare antithesis):

- *"Hospitality has a ceiling."* → what the carton buys → *"Bangla carries you further than English inside."*
- *"Information is freight; silence is ballast."* → told first → handshake as constitution
- *"Data walks. The bund holds."* → paper crosses borders → local copy of the miracle

**Connection leap (Eli — not a refrain, a scene shape):** plant unrelated facts in different registers → Eli names the bridge in one breath → someone reads **magic**, Emrys reads **hypothesis**, Wren reads **useful, not jurisdiction**. See `15` § *Uncanny connection*.

**Revision test:** read aloud on one breath. If you run out of air, cut.

## Revision checklist (per major chapter)

1. Does this chapter earn **one** sticky line (dialogue or cold narrator verdict)?
2. Is it spoken by a character with a **voice job**?
3. Does the next line **avoid explaining** it?
4. Does it pass the **misquote test** (slightly wrong wording still works)?
5. Is it already in the **refrain bible** or a one-off? (One-offs OK; don't compete with the 12)
