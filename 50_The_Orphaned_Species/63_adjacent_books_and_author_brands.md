## 1. Two-brand author model — LOCKED

**You are one human author under at most two public names.** No per-book
pseudonyms. No roster of “AI authors.” AI may assist drafting/research under
your control; the public promise is a person and a house style.

| Brand | Shelf | What it publishes | Name status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brand A — Literary / speculative** | Deep-history conspiracy thriller; literary speculative | *The Orphaned Species* Books I–IV (civic); V–X (Lifetime Orchestra / metaphysics) if published under this brand | **Working default: Rez Khan** (or your legal/preferred literary name). Confirm before query/agent materials. |
| **Brand B — Romance / intimate** | Spicy queer romance; intimate Weaver/route fiction | Wren/Rowan satellite(s) and any other spice/intimacy-first books set in this world | **OPEN — choose one romance pseudonym.** Do not announce until cover/query ready. |

**Hard rules**

1. **Two brands max** for this world-family. A third brand only if you later
   launch a truly separate genre (e.g. pure YA) — not for each satellite.
2. **No fake AI author identities** (invented bios, multiple model personas as
   “writers”). That fragments trust and muddies rights/voice.
3. **Disclosure:** if a platform or contract requires AI-assistance disclosure,
   disclose under *your* brand policy — do not invent a separate AI novelist.
4. **Cross-linking:** Brand B books may say “set in the world of *The Orphaned
   Species*” in back matter; Brand A need not soft-pedal Wren/Rowan to protect B.
5. **Canon wins:** satellites may heighten intimacy and spice; they may not
   contradict locked main-series facts.

### How to run “me under 1–2 names” in practice

1. **Pick Brand A name** (literary) — usually your real name or the name you
   already use for serious nonfiction/fiction (`Rez Khan` is the working default
   from Research / companion books).
2. **Pick Brand B name** (romance) — a stable pseudonym: searchable, ownable
   domain/social handle, not a joke that ages badly. Keep legal contracts in
   your real name; imprint/storefront shows the pseudonym.
3. **Separate storefront surfaces:** different Amazon/Kobo author pages, mailing
   lists, and cover languages. Same bank/tax identity behind the curtain.
4. **One style sheet per brand:** Brand A = `14` literary speculative register;
   Brand B = hotter, more interior, romance-beat structure, still this world’s
   consent grammar (`19`).
5. **Shared bible, split drafts:** both brands read `WORLD_BIBLE.md` /
   `SERIES_STRUCTURE.md` / `19`; Brand B manuscripts live under a clear folder
   (suggested: `50_The_Orphaned_Species/satellites/` when drafting begins).
6. **Agent/query:** pitch Brand A as the primary series; mention Brand B only if
   the agent handles romance or you want a dual-submission plan. Do not confuse
   a literary query with a spicy romance query.
