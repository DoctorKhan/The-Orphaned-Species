## Overall Assessment

The manuscript is a strong, coherent draft that respects its most critical structural locks: simultaneity at climax, decentralized-modular outcome, Eli's second relinquishment before first breath, Wren/Rowan/Eli distinct dyads, Costa Rica's refusal and Yuliana Mora, tree-as-infrastructure, work-song transmission, no children as emergency labor, and the Sideways hint in coda. The two clearest missed beats are the **on-page Heather/Union bloc-cube** and the **clean marriage offer aimed at Rowan**. The **southern-evacuation proof** is deferred rather than dramatized in Chapter 4. The prose is internally consistent; no POV drift, no geographic contradiction in the North American court/Costa Rica Turning, no clock collapse around pregnancy or transfer timing.
